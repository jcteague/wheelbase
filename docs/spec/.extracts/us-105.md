# Extract: us-105

## Summary

"Add the PMCC LEAPS roll: a pure `rollPmccLeaps` lifecycle rule set (phase, positive money, US-102's
coverage comparator from the long side, the strike-below-short shape rule, no-change — the two shape
rules skipped when no short call is open, and no "on or after current" rule), a service that writes
the **inverted** linked `ROLL_FROM` SELL / `ROLL_TO` BUY pair and calls US-103's `appendPmccSnapshot`
with `'PMCC_LEAPS_ROLL'` in one transaction, the `positions:roll-pmcc-leaps` channel, and the
`Roll LEAPS` sheet from the consolidated detail mockup — coverage ruler, live `Net Debit / Net
Credit` preview with the year-bearing roll label and replacement DTE, post-roll basis, LEAPS cost to
date and the **re-anchored** effective share cost, the advisory above-short-strike warning and the
advisory shortened-coverage note." Linear story: OPT-13 / US-105; estimated 5 points. Layout of
record: `mockups/epic-09-pmcc-position-detail.mdx` (`RollLeapsSheet`, presets `rl-entry` …
`rl-no-short`); `mockups/us-105-roll-pmcc-leaps.mdx` is design history and carries the Linear
figures. **No migration.** Built on `main` after US-103 and US-104 merged, generalizing US-104's
shared pieces in place. (Source: `plans/us-105/plan.md` § Summary.)

**Implementation superseded the plan in places (the code wins; recorded here so the spec reflects
what was built):** the LEAPS roll's cross-field rules became one ordered pure list
`pmccLeapsRollIssues` in `src/main/core/pmcc-rules.ts`, consumed by both the engine and the renderer
schema, and it gained three date rules the plan did not have (fill on/before the LEAPS expiration,
fill on/after the position's last recorded event — `before_last_event`, which replaced an earlier
`close_date_before_open` — and replacement expiration after the fill); `ROLL_NO_CHANGE` became a shared
constant; `RollResultBase` stayed phase-generic and gained `From`/`To` via a new `RollLegPair`; the
two PMCC roll services share one write path (`src/main/services/pmcc-roll-record.ts`); the two PMCC
roll sheets share a dialog frame, a fields grid and a submit hook; the engine results are named
`RollPmccLeapsAccepted` / `RollPmccShortCallAccepted`; ledgers with a known strike are typed
`PmccLedgerWithStrike`; and the positions-list PMCC row became roll- and
expiry-aware (the removed `pmccOpeningLegs`). All 15 e2e cases (one per AC scenario / outline row)
pass in `e2e/pmcc-leaps-roll.spec.ts`.

## Architecture Decisions

### ADR: US-105 builds on US-104 and generalizes its shared pieces in place

- **Decision:** Every main-process area imports US-103's `appendPmccSnapshot` / `groupRollChains` /
  `readLegs` and US-104's `pmccOpenLegs`; every renderer area reuses US-104's `pmcc-roll.ts`,
  `PmccCoverageRuler`, `FormButton` disabled treatment, `PositionDetailActions` PMCC branch,
  `usePositionDetailSheets` banner state and sheet skeleton. Where US-104's shape was short-roll
  specific it is generalized and US-104's call sites updated: `pmccRollNet` takes
  `{ received, paid, contracts }`; `pmccRollLabel(current, next, monthFormat)` takes `'MMM'` or
  `'MMM yyyy'` and gains the `In` direction; `PmccCoverageRuler` becomes marks-based; the banner
  state becomes a discriminated union over the two roll kinds.
- **Why:** Linear says the two rolls share "the sheet layout, coverage ruler, and roll-label helper"
  and whichever lands first extracts them. Generalizing by parameter is smaller than a second copy
  and keeps the wheel's `rolls.ts` untouched.
- **Alternatives considered:** Build standalone against `wb-103` (reverses the agreed order); reuse
  the wheel's `getCcRollTypeDetail` (wrong text shape); one `RollPmccSheet` with a `side` prop (the
  two sheets differ in sections, read-only context, which figure re-anchors and which checks skip).
- **Source:** `plans/us-105/research.md`

### ADR: `rollPmccLeaps` is a pure lifecycle function with a fixed rule order; no "on or after current" rule

- **Decision (as built):** `rollPmccLeaps(input: RollPmccLeapsInput): RollPmccLeapsAccepted`
  (`{ phase: 'PMCC_OPEN' }`) in `src/main/core/lifecycle.ts`; US-104's engine result is likewise
  renamed `RollPmccShortCallAccepted`, so neither clashes with the IPC result types in
  `schemas.ts`. Input: `currentPhase`, `currentLeaps: PmccLegTerms` (`strike`, `expiration`), `openShort: PmccLegTerms | null`, `lastEventDate` (from the pure
  `latestFillDate(legs)` in `pmcc-rules.ts`), `fillDate`, `newStrike`,
  `newExpiration`, `proceedsPerContract`, `replacementDebitPerContract`. Rules, first failure throws
  `ValidationError(field, code, message)`:

  | #   | Rule                                                    | field                         | code                          | message                                                       |
  | --- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | 1   | phase is not `PMCC_OPEN`                                | `__phase__`                   | `invalid_phase`               | `Position is not in PMCC_OPEN phase`                          |
  | 2   | proceeds ≤ 0                                            | `proceedsPerContract`         | `must_be_positive`            | `Proceeds from close must be greater than zero`               |
  | 3   | replacement debit ≤ 0                                   | `replacementDebitPerContract` | `must_be_positive`            | `Replacement debit must be greater than zero`                 |
  | 4   | new strike ≤ 0                                          | `newStrike`                   | `must_be_positive`            | `Strike must be positive`                                     |
  | 5   | fill date > current LEAPS expiration                    | `fillDate`                    | `close_date_after_expiration` | `Fill date cannot be after the LEAPS expiration date`         |
  | 6   | fill date < `lastEventDate`                             | `fillDate`                    | `before_last_event`           | `Fill date cannot be before the last recorded event (Jul 9, 2027)` |
  | 7   | open short and replacement does not outlast it (US-102) | `newExpiration`               | `long_not_after_short`        | `LEAPS call must expire after the short call.`                |
  | 8   | open short and replacement strike ≥ short strike        | `newStrike`                   | `strike_not_below_short`      | `LEAPS strike must be below the short-call strike ($112.00).` |
  | 9   | replacement expiration ≤ fill date                      | `newExpiration`               | `expiration_not_after_fill`   | `Expiration must be after the fill date.`                     |
  | 10  | strike and expiration both unchanged (`Decimal.eq`)     | `__roll__`                    | `no_change`                   | `Roll must change the strike, the expiration, or both`        |

  Rules 1–4 are the engine's own guards (`requirePmccOpenPhase`, `requirePositiveDecimal`,
  `requirePositiveStrike`); rules 5–10 come from `pmccLeapsRollIssues`. Rules 7–8 are **skipped when
  `openShort` is null**. There is deliberately **no** `must_be_on_or_after_current` rule: a
  replacement earlier than the current LEAPS is accepted (advisory note in the renderer). No logging,
  no I/O.
- **Why:** Mirrors `rollPmccShortCall`'s shape (phase → money → shared ordered rules) so the two PMCC
  rolls read the same and the IPC error table gains no synonyms. Coverage precedes strike because it
  is the naked-call safety rule; both precede no-change. Rules 5, 6 and 9 came out of TDD / review:
  a roll after the LEAPS expired sells a contract that no longer exists; a roll back-dated before a
  later leg would sort before it, leaving the latest snapshot stale and having the shape rules judge
  today's short rather than the one open on the fill date (rule 6 replaced an earlier
  `close_date_before_open` against the LEAPS' own fill; the last event is never before that fill, so
  it covers it — the LEAPS roll has no `close_date_before_open`); an already-expired replacement
  would be recorded as the open LEAPS forever.
- **Alternatives considered:** A `side` flag on `rollCc` or `rollPmccShortCall`; coverage check only
  in the service; a discriminated union for `openShort`.
- **Source:** `plans/us-105/research.md` (rule table, as amended by implementation)

### ADR: The LEAPS roll's rules are one ordered pure list shared by engine and form

- **Decision:** `pmccLeapsRollIssues(input: PmccLeapsRollInput): PmccIssue<PmccLeapsRollField>[]` in
  `src/main/core/pmcc-rules.ts` returns every broken rule 5–10 in order. `PmccLeapsRollInput`
  extends `PmccLeapsRollContext` (`currentLeaps`, `openShort`, `lastEventDate`) with `fillDate`, `newStrike`,
  `newExpiration`, each `undefined` while undecided (the rule that reads it is skipped).
  `PmccLeapsRollField` = `'fillDate' | 'newStrike' | 'newExpiration'` (derived from
  `RollPmccLeapsInput`'s keys) `| '__roll__'`. The engine throws the first issue; the renderer
  schema (`src/renderer/src/schemas/roll-pmcc-leaps.ts`) reports the first issue per path from the
  same call. `RollPmccLeapsContext = PmccLeapsRollContext`. `latestFillDate(legs)` returns the
  latest leg fill date (`''` with no legs); the service and `PositionDetailPage` call it.
- **Why:** Same as US-104's `pmccShortRollIssues` — one home for codes and messages, so the form
  rejects with the engine's text before the round-trip and cannot drift. The plan had the form
  spread individual constants in its own `superRefine`; implementation folded every cross-field rule
  into one list instead.
- **Source:** implementation (`src/main/core/pmcc-rules.ts`); plan § Area 6 superseded

### ADR: The long-side half of US-102 and the strike-ordering predicate live in `pmcc-rules.ts`

- **Decision:** `pmcc-rules.ts` exports, under a `// US-102 — PMCC coverage comparator` block:
  `shortExpiresBeforeLeaps` (one predicate), `SHORT_NOT_BEFORE_LONG` (short side) and
  `LONG_NOT_AFTER_SHORT = { code: 'long_not_after_short', message: 'LEAPS call must expire after the short call.' }`
  (long side) — no second predicate; the long side calls `shortExpiresBeforeLeaps(openShort.expiration, newExpiration)`.
  Also `shortStrikeAboveLeaps(shortStrike, longStrike)` (`Decimal.gt`), now used by
  `pmccCrossLegIssues`'s `strike_not_above_long` rule; `STRIKE_NOT_BELOW_SHORT_CODE =
'strike_not_below_short'`; `leapsStrikeNotBelowShortMessage(shortStrike)` (formats `$112.00`); and
  `ROLL_NO_CHANGE` (`no_change`, shared by both PMCC rolls); `latestFillDate`.
- **Why:** US-102: "centralize the pure comparison … rather than leaving a second inline copy on each
  roll path". The renderer already imports this module.
- **Alternatives considered:** Hard-code the inverse message in `lifecycle.ts`; a named alias
  `leapsExpiresAfterShort` (a second function for one comparison).
- **Source:** `plans/us-105/research.md`

### ADR: `rollPmccLeapsPosition` writes the inverted pair through a shared PMCC roll writer

- **Decision:** `src/main/services/roll-pmcc-leaps-position.ts` exports
  `rollPmccLeapsPosition(db, positionId, payload)`, wrapped in `logPmccRollRejection`. Flow:
  `loadPmccRollContext` (`getPosition` → `not_found`, then `requirePmccOpenLegs(detail)` →
  `__phase__` / `invalid_phase` for a non-PMCC_OPEN row, else `__root__` / `no_active_leg`), money
  to 4 dp (`money4`, now in `leg-rows.ts`), `newStrike` defaults to the open LEAPS strike,
  `lastEventDate = latestFillDate(detail.legs)`, `rollPmccLeaps(...)`, then `recordPmccRoll(db, positionId, { from: SELL current LEAPS @ proceeds,
to: BUY replacement @ debit, contracts: long.contracts, fillDate, triggerEvent: 'PMCC_LEAPS_ROLL' })`.
  `recordPmccRoll`, `requirePmccOpenLegs`, `loadPmccRollContext` and `logPmccRollRejection` live in
  the new `src/main/services/pmcc-roll-record.ts` and are shared with `rollPmccShortCallPosition`: one `db.transaction` inserts both legs via
  `insertLegRow` (fresh `roll_chain_id`, `fees '0.0000'`, `instrument_type 'CALL'`, fill = premium)
  and calls `appendPmccSnapshot`. The position row and any open short are untouched.
  `leapsRollNumber = nextPmccRollNumber(detail.legs, 'leaps')` (`pmcc-ledger.ts`; one plus the
  long-side chains, i.e. those that are not `isShortCallRoll`), shared with the short roll's
  `'short'` count.
- **Why:** The story: "writes ROLL_FROM + ROLL_TO + the cost-basis snapshot in one transaction …
  lets US-103 own the formula". `groupRollChains` nets by action and `currentLeapsLeg` picks the
  SELL-chain's `ROLL_TO`, so the inverted pair needs no flag downstream (ledger `19.3000`,
  `leapsCostPerShare` `22.5000`, `effectiveShareCost` `104.3000`). The plan's Refactor suggested the
  shared writer "only if both services' tests stay green" — it was extracted.
- **Alternatives considered:** Fees on roll legs; `LEAPS_CLOSE` + new `LEAPS_OPEN` (breaks linked
  pairs).
- **Source:** `plans/us-105/research.md`; implementation

### ADR: A dedicated payload schema and a pair-generic `RollResultBase`

- **Decision:** `RollPmccLeapsPayloadSchema = z.object({ positionId, proceedsPerContract: positive, replacementDebitPerContract: positive, newExpiration: IsoDateSchema, newStrike?: positive, fillDate?: IsoDateSchema })`
  — not `RollPayloadBaseSchema`. New `RollLegPair<From extends LegAction = 'BUY', To extends LegAction = 'SELL'>`
  (`rollFromLeg`, `rollToLeg`, `rollChainId`); `RollResultBase<Phase, From = 'BUY', To = 'SELL'>`
  extends it and owns `position` + `costBasisSnapshot`. `RollPmccLeapsResult extends
RollResultBase<'PMCC_OPEN', 'SELL', 'BUY'>` narrows the snapshot to `triggerEvent:
'PMCC_LEAPS_ROLL'; finalPnl: null` and adds `ledger: PmccLedgerWithStrike`, `leapsRollNumber`.
  `PmccLedgerWithStrike` (`src/main/core/costbasis.ts`) is `PmccLedgerResult` with a non-null
  `effectiveShareCost`, returned by the `calculatePmccLedger` overload that takes a `longStrike`;
  `pmccLedgerFromLegs` and `AppendPmccSnapshotResult.ledger` use it too.
  `recordPmccRoll`'s result is `AppendPmccSnapshotResult<T> & RollLegPair<From, To>`.
- **Why:** The AC's field-level errors name proceeds and a debit; the wire names should say what the
  money is. The generic pair derives the inverted legs from the shipped type. (Plan had
  `RollResultBase<From, To>` without the phase; the US-104 `Phase` parameter was kept.)
- **Source:** `plans/us-105/research.md`; implementation

### ADR: The sheet is the consolidated mockup's `RollLeapsSheet`; both advisories are renderer-side

- **Decision:** `RollPmccLeapsSheet` (inside the shared `PmccRollSheetDialog`, `aria-label="Roll
LEAPS"`, required props include `lastEventDate`) owns the RHF instance from
  `useRollPmccLeapsForm({ leaps, openShort, lastEventDate }, today)` (`mode: 'onChange'`,
  `zodResolver`) and
  submits through the shared `useRollSheetSubmit`. `RollPmccLeapsForm` renders, top to bottom:
  Coverage (ruler from `leapsRollCoverage`), Current LEAPS (contract, expiration/DTE, `Bought at`,
  `LEAPS cost to date`, `Cost basis`, highlighted `Effective share cost`, re-anchors note), Open
  short call (read-only, or `No short call open — nothing to cover, so the expiration and strike
checks are skipped.`), Replacement LEAPS inputs (`RollPmccLeapsFields` over the shared
  `PmccRollFields` grid, which owns the RHF `deps` rules for both PMCC sheets), the Net card
  (`RollPmccLeapsNetPreview`) or dashed placeholder, the advisory warning
  (`pmcc-leaps-roll-warning`), the advisory shortened-coverage note
  (`pmcc-leaps-roll-coverage-note`), the no-change error and the footer. Confirm is disabled on
  `schema.safeParse(values)` failure (US-104 convention), not RHF `isValid`. The derived figures
  come from the pure `leapsRollView(values, ctx)` in `src/renderer/src/lib/pmcc-leaps-roll-view.ts`. The fill-date input
  declares `deps: 'newExpiration'` and strike/expiration declare `deps: '__roll__'`.
- **Why:** The story: warning and coverage notes are advisory and renderer-side (ADR
  `soft-client-side-warnings`). `mode: 'onChange'` makes "confirm is disabled" true while typing.
- **Source:** `plans/us-105/research.md`; implementation

### ADR: The net preview and the roll label are the AC's exact strings; the LEAPS label carries the year

- **Decision:** `pmcc-leaps-roll-net` renders `Net Credit: $2.50 per share ($250.00 total)` /
  `Net Debit: …` (grouped thousands, `$1,100.00`). `pmcc-leaps-roll-label` renders
  `pmccRollLabel(current, next, 'MMM yyyy')` (`Roll Up & Out: $80 to $85 strike, Sep 2027 to Jan 2028
expiration`, `Roll Out: same $80 strike, …`, `Roll In: …` for an earlier replacement) plus
  ` · N DTE`. `pmccRollNet` returns `{ net: Decimal (signed), perShare, total }`. A zero net reads
  `Net Even:`. The card's closing sentence: debit `You are buying time value; it lands in the
  basis.`; credit at the same or lower strike `Cash came out; it lowers the basis.`; credit at a
  higher strike `Cash came out, but the strike moved up more than the credit.`; even `No net cash
  changed hands.` The above-short-strike warning is exactly the AC sentence (`… If assigned,
  covering from the LEAPS realizes roughly $0.80 per share of loss.`).
- **Source:** `plans/us-105/research.md`; implementation (`RollPmccLeapsNetPreview.tsx`,
  `RollPmccLeapsForm.tsx`)

### ADR: Success closes the sheet; one banner slot for both rolls

- **Decision:** `usePositionDetailSheets` holds one `RollSheet` slot (`'csp' | 'cc' | 'pmccShort' |
'pmccLeaps' | null`) and one `PmccRecordedRoll | null` banner state
  (`{ kind: 'short' | 'leaps'; result }`, in `src/renderer/src/lib/pmcc-roll-recorded.ts`). `PmccRollRecordedBanner({ recorded })` renders both kinds;
  the LEAPS banner (`pmcc-leaps-roll-recorded-banner`) reads `Rolled — sold to close XYZ $80.00 CALL
<exp> at $26.00, bought to open XYZ $85.00 CALL <exp> at $23.50. LEAPS roll #1 credit · $2.50 per
share ($250.00 total). Both legs share one roll chain. Effective share cost if exercised: $104.30 —
the $85.00 strike plus $19.30 basis.`
- **Why:** The AC's "history shows one line 'LEAPS roll #1 credit'" is US-109's timeline; until it
  ships the banner names the line. A single slot makes two banners (or two open roll sheets)
  unrepresentable. The plan's Refactor suggested folding the four roll booleans — done.
- **Source:** `plans/us-105/research.md`; implementation

Boolean(pmccOpenLegs)`. The sheet mounts only when `pmccLeapsLedger(pmccLedger)` is non-null (the

- **Source:** `plans/us-105/research.md`

### ADR: The positions-list PMCC row reads the open legs

- **Decision (implementation, not in the plan):** `listPositions`' `readPmccSummaries` derives
  `long` / `short` from `pmccOpenLegs(legs)` (roll- and expiry-aware), not from the removed
  `pmccOpeningLegs` ("later rows win, rolls not consulted"). `PmccListSummary.short` is nullable;
  `PmccPositionRow` renders `$80.00 / No short open` and omits the short expiration/DTE lines; the list orders a
  no-short PMCC by its LEAPS DTE (`position-order.ts`: `(pmcc.short ?? pmcc.long).dte`). Follow-up
  US-123 (Linear OPT-29) adds a `NO_SHORT_OPEN` alert and sorts a no-short PMCC as DTE 0.
- **Why:** Once a LEAPS roll is recorded, the opening `LEAPS_OPEN` no longer describes the position;
  and after a short expires the list would have shown a dead short. The list and the detail page now
  read one answer.
- **Source:** implementation (`src/main/services/list-positions.ts`, `src/main/core/position-order.ts`)

## Contracts

### `positions:roll-pmcc-leaps`

- **Type:** IPC handler
- **Shape:**

  ```typescript
  // Zod schema: RollPmccLeapsPayloadSchema
  {
    positionId: string                     // a PMCC in PMCC_OPEN with an open LEAPS
    proceedsPerContract: number            // positive — sell-to-close price per share of the current LEAPS
    replacementDebitPerContract: number    // positive — buy-to-open price per share of the replacement
    newExpiration: string                  // YYYY-MM-DD; > open short's expiration (when one is open); > fillDate
    newStrike?: number                     // positive; < open short's strike (when one is open); defaults to the open LEAPS strike
    fillDate?: string                      // YYYY-MM-DD (ISO-checked); defaults to today; ≤ LEAPS expiration, ≥ last recorded event
  }
  // success
  {
    ok: true,
    position: { id; ticker; phase: 'PMCC_OPEN'; status: 'ACTIVE' },
    rollFromLeg: LegRecord & { legRole: 'ROLL_FROM'; action: 'SELL' },  // SELL CALL $80 @ 26.0000, fees '0.0000'
    rollToLeg:   LegRecord & { legRole: 'ROLL_TO';   action: 'BUY' },   // BUY CALL $85 @ 23.5000, fees '0.0000'
    rollChainId: string,
    costBasisSnapshot: CostBasisSnapshotRecord & { triggerEvent: 'PMCC_LEAPS_ROLL'; finalPnl: null }, // 19.3000, 320.0000
    ledger: PmccLedgerWithStrike,         // basisTotal 1930.0000, leapsCostPerShare 22.5000, effectiveShareCost 104.3000
    leapsRollNumber: number               // long-side roll chains including this one
  }
  ```

  Errors, first failure returned: any Zod issue; `__root__` / `not_found`; `__phase__` /
  `invalid_phase` (also for a wheel id); `__root__` / `no_active_leg`; engine rules 2–10 above;
  `__root__` / `internal_error` on a storage failure (nothing written).

- **Source:** `plans/us-105/contracts/positions-roll-pmcc-leaps.md` (error table amended by the
  three fill-date rules)
- **Implementation:** `src/main/ipc/positions.ts` (`registerParsedPositionHandler(db,
'positions:roll-pmcc-leaps', 'positions_roll_pmcc_leaps_unhandled_error',
RollPmccLeapsPayloadSchema, rollPmccLeapsPosition)`), `src/main/services/roll-pmcc-leaps-position.ts`,
  `src/main/services/pmcc-roll-record.ts`; preload `rollPmccLeaps` / `IpcRollPmccLeapsResult`;
  adapter `src/renderer/src/api/positions.ts#rollPmccLeaps` (`RollPmccLeapsPayload =
IpcPayload<'rollPmccLeaps'>`, `RollPmccLeapsResponse = IpcSuccess<'rollPmccLeaps'>`); hook
  `useRollPmccLeaps`. The sheet routes `proceedsPerContract → proceeds`,
  `replacementDebitPerContract → newDebit`, `newStrike`, `newExpiration`, `fillDate`, `__roll__`;
  any other field's error shows its own message in `root.save` (`showUnplacedMessages: true`); a
  failure with no field errors shows `Could not record the roll. Your entries are preserved. Try
  again.`

## Acceptance Criteria

Linear OPT-13 (Background: valuation 2027-07-09; XYZ PMCC, $80 LEAPS exp 2027-09-17 bought at
$25.00, 1 contract; open $112 short exp 2027-07-16 sold at $2.40; basis $21.80, effective share cost
$101.80):

- The roll form shows the decision context for the open LEAPS ($80.00, 70 DTE, $25.00; the $112 short
  as read-only coverage context; $21.80 / $101.80; inputs for proceeds, strike, expiration, debit,
  fill date).
- Net credit preview for a roll up and out (26.00 → $85 2028-01-21 @ 23.50: `Net Credit: $2.50 per
share ($250.00 total)`, `Roll Up & Out: $80 to $85 strike, Sep 2027 to Jan 2028 expiration`, 196
  DTE, basis $19.30, effective $104.30 up from $101.80, named as $85.00 + $19.30).
- Net debit preview for a roll out at the same strike (@ 29.20: `Net Debit: $3.20 per share ($320.00
total)`, `Roll Out: same $80 strike, …`, basis $25.00, effective $105.00, confirm enabled).
- Recording the roll creates a linked leg pair and one net cost-basis line (ROLL_FROM SELL $80 @
  $26.00, ROLL_TO BUY $85 @ $23.50, one chain id, basis $19.30, LEAPS cost to date $22.50, one line
  "LEAPS roll #1 credit" $2.50, effective $104.30, the $112 short unchanged).
- Warning when the effective share cost after the roll rises above the open short strike (@ 37.00 to
  2029-01-19: `Net Debit: $11.00 per share ($1,100.00 total)`, warning naming $112.80 and $0.80;
  confirm enabled).
- Outline: Reject a replacement strike not below the open short strike ($112.00, $115.00): `LEAPS
strike must be below the short-call strike ($112.00).`; confirm disabled.
- Outline: Reject a replacement expiration that would leave the short call uncovered (2027-07-16,
  2027-07-02): US-102's message beside the expiration; no legs recorded; $80 LEAPS stays open.
- Allow a replacement that shortens coverage but still outlasts the short call (2027-08-20 @ 26.60:
  `Net Debit: $0.60 …`; advisory 42 DTE, 28 days shorter; confirm enabled).
- Reject a roll that changes neither strike nor expiration.
- Outline: Reject non-positive proceeds (`Proceeds from close must be greater than zero`) or
  replacement debit (`Replacement debit must be greater than zero`).
- Roll the LEAPS while no short call is open (after the $112 short expires: roll recorded at $19.30;
  no short-strike or long-vs-short check; `No short call open` in place of the coverage context).
- Leave a classic wheel unaffected (no `Roll LEAPS` on a `CC_OPEN` wheel; its roll paths unchanged).

Out of scope per story: the "LEAPS roll #1 credit" timeline line (US-109; the banner names it until
then); fees on roll legs.

## Decisions & Tradeoffs

- **Worked figures** (1 contract, $0 fees; basis 21.80, LEAPS cost 25.00, eff 101.80):
  `basis after = 21.80 − net`, `LEAPS cost after = 25.00 − net`, `effective after = newStrike + basis
after`. Up & out +2.50 → 19.30 / 22.50 / 104.30; out −3.20 → 25.00 / 28.20 / 105.00; too much time
  −11.00 → 32.80 / 36.00 / 112.80 (shortfall 0.80); shortens −0.60 → 22.40 / 25.60 / 102.40. After
  the up-and-out roll the snapshot chain reads `23.0000, 23.0000, 21.2000, 21.8000, 19.3000`
  (`PMCC_OPEN`, `PMCC_SHORT_EXPIRED`, `PMCC_SHORT_OPEN`, `PMCC_SHORT_ROLL`, `PMCC_LEAPS_ROLL`);
  `totalPremiumCollected` stays `320.0000`. (Source: `plans/us-105/research.md`.)
- **Renderer post-roll helper:** `pmccLeapsPostRoll({ ledger, newStrike, contracts, net })` returns
  `postBasis`, `effectiveAfter` (re-anchored on the replacement strike) and `leapsCostAfter`,
  computed by the core `calculatePmccLedger` over the LEAPS cost so far (LONG), the short credits so
  far (SHORT) and the roll's net (LONG); `pmccLeapsLedger(ledger)` narrows the detail ledger to a
  known effective share cost;
  `effectiveCostMove(before, after)` renders `up $2.50 from $101.80` / `down …` / `unchanged`;
  `leapsCoverageChange(current, next)` drives the shortened-coverage note.
- **Coverage ruler** is marks-based: `PmccCoverageRuler` takes a `PmccRollCoverage` (`blocked`,
  `fillTo`, `marks: PmccRulerMark[]` with a `PmccRulerTone`, `note`) plus `today`, built by pure
  `shortRollCoverage` / `leapsRollCoverage` in
  `src/renderer/src/lib/pmcc-roll-coverage.ts`. A mark with `date: null` renders a legend row with
  no tick (`Open short call · none`).
- **Fill-date input re-checks the expiration** (`deps: 'newExpiration'`), since rule 9 reads both.
- **The LEAPS banner names the effective share cost if exercised**, built from the response's
  `ledger` and `rollToLeg.strike`.
- **The sheet's `__root__` / `__phase__` errors show their own message** (both PMCC sheets route
  with `showUnplacedMessages: true`).
- **The shortened-coverage note shows only once the replacement expiration parses**, so its DTE is
  never `null`.
- **Money crosses the IPC as numbers**, formatted to 4 dp in the service (`money4`), like every roll.
