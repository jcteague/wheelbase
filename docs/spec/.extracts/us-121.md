# Extract: us-121

## Summary

"Barchart is unreachable, so IV rank is rebuilt from data we are entitled to: a pure engine in
`src/main/core/` inverts European Black–Scholes over Alpaca **daily option-bar VWAP** against the
underlying's **SIP daily VWAP**, selects strikes and bracketing expirations with numbered gates,
and interpolates to a 30-day IV in total variance. The series is persisted per session **with its
inputs** (`iv30_reading`) and gaps are recorded (`iv30_gap`); rank, percentile and the 52-week
range are derived on read over the 252 sessions before the latest reading, published only at
≥ 200 coverage." The existing `ivr-collect` job, `ivr:collect-now` channel, watchlist-add trigger,
freshness tiers, `IvrCell` and screener floor keep working on the new value; the Barchart scraper,
its fake, its e2e seam and its `ivr_snapshot` table are deleted (migration 016); the e2e seam is
replaced by a fake provider that synthesises bars from a programmed IV series. An absent rank says
why: every bench row and ranked candidate carries exactly one of `ivRank` / `ivRankAbsence`
(`pending` / `insufficient_history` / `no_market_data` / `failed` / `not_collected`); the reason is
display-only and never reaches the verdict or the screener floor. Linear story: OPT-27 / US-121.
(Source: `plans/us-121/plan.md` Summary; `docs/us-121-implementation.md`.)

## Architecture Decisions

### ADR: IV30 is inverted from daily bar VWAP against a stored constant rate

- **Decision:** For each session and each selected expiration, price = the option's daily bar `vw`,
  underlying = the SIP daily bar `vw` of the same session. Invert European Black–Scholes (no
  dividends, `r = 0.045`, `q = 0`, stored with every reading) for the call and the put at the chosen
  strike, average the two, then interpolate the two expirations to exactly 30 days in **total
  variance** (`w = σ²T`, `σ30 = sqrt(w30 / T30)`). Today's reading, the backfill and the catch-up all
  run this one engine; Alpaca's per-contract `impliedVolatility` is never used for rank.
- **Why:** Alpaca has no historical option quotes, so the earlier closing-mid / implied-forward
  design has no input. Rank is self-relative, so the series must be consistent with itself; mixing
  inverted history with vendor IV for today would rank apples against oranges (spike: the two
  methods disagreed on AAPL's 52-week high by 10 rank points). Daily VWAP agreed with near-close
  minute bars to within half a vol point at a tenth of the request cost.
- **Alternatives considered:** near-close 1-minute bars (10× requests, rate-limited, 9–43% of
  sessions incomplete); hour bars; implied forward from simultaneous call/put quotes (none exist); a
  rate series (deferred — the stored rate makes it a recompute, not a refetch).
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`

### ADR: Persist the IV30 series with typed inputs; derive rank, percentile and range on read

- **Decision:** New table `iv30_reading` keyed `(underlying, session, method)`, one row per session
  holding `iv30` and everything that produced it (underlying VWAP, tier, near/far expiration, strike
  per expiration, four option VWAPs and trade counts, rate, dividend yield, `engine_version`,
  `observed_at` = the session's close). Rank, percentile and 52-week range are pure functions of the
  series (`core/iv-metrics.ts`) computed on every read; nothing derived is stored. `ivr_snapshot` is
  dropped.
- **Why:** The engine will be wrong at least once; Alpaca's history window rolls forward; "why did
  AAPL read 25 that day?" must be answerable. Typed columns validate and query better than JSON.
  Deriving on read is what makes a corrected engine correct every metric at once.
- **Alternatives considered:** store rank alongside (drift risk); versioned JSON inputs; reuse
  `ivr_snapshot` with new columns (wrong PK, mixed provenance).
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`, `plans/us-121/data-model.md`

### ADR: A session that cannot produce a reading is recorded as a gap — except the newest one

- **Decision:** `iv30_gap (underlying, session, method, reason, attempted_at)` records every session
  tried and not read (`no_underlying_bar`, `no_tradeable_pair`). A gap never contributes a value,
  counts against the 200-of-252 coverage gate, and stops the next run re-probing. The most recent
  completed session is never written as a gap — it is retried next run.
- **Why:** Missing sessions must be computable without refetching (ETSY: 16 gap sessions ≈ 7
  wasted requests a night otherwise). A wrongly recorded gap on a lagging newest bar would suppress
  that session forever.
- **Alternatives considered:** nullable `iv30` on `iv30_reading`; no gap table with unbounded
  re-probing; scheduled gap retries (Alpaca does not revise daily bars).
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`

### ADR: The rank window is the 252 completed sessions strictly before the anchor session

- **Decision:** Anchor = the session of the ticker's latest stored reading. Window = the 252
  sessions immediately preceding it on the cached exchange calendar; the anchor is ranked against
  the window but not part of it. Published only when ≥ 200 of the 252 hold a reading; otherwise
  rank, percentile, low and high all read `n/a`. `rank = (today − low) / (high − low) × 100`
  clamped to `[0, 100]`, `null` when `high === low`; `percentile = count(window readings strictly
  below today) / count(window) × 100`; both round half-up to an integer via `Decimal`.
  `observedAt` = the anchor session's close, so US-98 tiers age a stale anchor as before.
- **Why:** Every scenario's arithmetic excludes today from its own range (0.2475 in 0.18–0.45 → 25;
  0.47 clamps to 100 while high still reports 0.45). Rank is a min/max statistic — one missed spike
  day corrupts the max for a year — so coverage is not cosmetic. Anchoring on the latest reading
  keeps a stale series legible (and marked stale).
- **Alternatives considered:** 52 calendar weeks; including today in the window; withholding only
  rank on sparse coverage.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`

### ADR: `AssessedIvRank.value` becomes nullable; percentile, low and high travel with it

- **Decision:** `IvRankReading = { value: string | null; percentile; low; high; observedAt }`;
  `AssessedIvRank` adds `ageTradingDays` and `state`. `value` is the integer rank as a string or
  `null` for a flat window. `IpcIvRank` and the renderer's `IvRank` (`api/ivr.ts`) mirror it. Core screener `IvRank` is
  unchanged — `usableIvRanks` also drops `value === null`, so `iv_rank_floor` never sees null.
  `ivGate` treats null as `unknown('IV unavailable')`.
- **Why:** "A flat window withholds rank but not percentile" needs a reading with percentile and
  range but `n/a` rank; widening the field is a three-file ripple.
- **Alternatives considered:** separate `ivMetrics` field; drop the flat-window scenario.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`

### ADR: Daily bars are two `MarketDataProvider` capabilities; the adapter hides batching and paging

- **Decision:** `getOptionDailyBars({ symbols, start, end? })` → `Map<symbol, DailyBar[]>` and
  `getStockDailyBars({ symbol, start, end? })` → `DailyBar[]`, `DailyBar = { date, vwap, close,
  volume, tradeCount }` with `date` the Eastern session day. The Alpaca adapter chunks 100 symbols
  per request, follows `next_page_token`, uses `feed=sip` for stocks, and emits `end` only when the
  caller supplies one. `IvHistoryBarSource` is the `Pick` the service takes. Never on `BrokerProvider`.
- **Why:** CLAUDE.md port rule — one capability, one port; the adapter hides how many calls it takes.
- **Alternatives considered:** single `getDailyBars(kind, …)`; a separate `IvHistorySource` port
  (the split US-116 undid).
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`, `plans/us-121/contracts/market-data-provider-daily-bars.md`

### ADR: The service, not the adapter, guarantees "never name today as `end`"

- **Decision:** `collectIvHistory` sets `end` to the newest completed session's date when that date
  is before `etDateOf(now)`, and omits `end` when the newest completed session is today. The adapter
  passes `end` verbatim. (Alpaca returns `403 OPRA agreement is not signed` when `end` is the
  current calendar day.)
- **Why:** Only the service holds calendar and clock; an adapter "strip today" guard would silently
  change a request instead of making the caller correct.
- **Alternatives considered:** always omit `end` (hundreds of unwanted bars per symbol); clamp in the
  adapter.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`

### ADR: Contract selection is generic over candidate expirations; one-sided brackets do not extrapolate

- **Decision:** `selectExpirationPair` filters candidates to DTE ≥ 7, picks the largest ≤ 30 and the
  smallest > 30; exactly 30 → alone; one side only → nearest alone (`far: null`), no extrapolation.
  Weekly candidates = Fridays in `[session+7, session+45]`, monthly = third Fridays in
  `[session+7, session+70]`, each shifted to the prior session on a closure. Weekly tier first;
  monthly when either weekly expiration finds no qualifying strike. Strikes = floor/ceil of the
  underlying VWAP at increments 0.5, 1, 2.5, 5, deduplicated, nearest first. A strike qualifies when
  both legs have a bar with `tradeCount ≥ 1` **and** both invert; otherwise the next-nearest is tried.
- **Why:** The near-expiry scenario outline speaks in listed expirations; treating an inversion
  failure like an untraded leg is the smaller rule and yields fewer gaps; one-sided variance
  extrapolation is a spike shortcut, not a method.
- **Alternatives considered:** hard-coded "two Fridays around D+30"; gap on first inversion failure;
  linear extrapolation.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`

### ADR: The closed-day guard is removed; idempotence over missing sessions replaces it

- **Decision:** `collectIvHistoryBatch` (formerly `collectIVRSnapshots`) no longer takes a `trigger` and no longer consults the calendar
  for permission. Each ticker's run fetches only required sessions with neither reading nor gap; on
  a weekend/holiday that set is empty → `up_to_date`, no bar request. `skippedReason` loses
  `'market_closed'`. `JobRunContext.trigger` stays on the scheduler, unconsumed. Supersedes the
  `ivr-non-trading-day-guard-in-collector` ADR and its US-100 amendment.
- **Why:** The guard stopped a Saturday run re-scraping Friday's Barchart number; with bars the run
  knows what it holds, so a closed-day run is a no-op by construction.
- **Alternatives considered:** keep the guard for scheduled runs only; skip the weekly calendar
  refresh on closures.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`, `plans/us-121/contracts/ivr-collect-now.md`

### ADR: A market-data auth failure aborts the run as a skip; every other failure is per-ticker

- **Decision:** A `MarketDataError('auth_failed')` turn returns `{ 0, 0, 0, skippedReason:
  'market_data_unavailable' }` with one INFO line (`ivr_collection_skipped_no_market_data`) and marks
  every remaining target `no_market_data`. Any other error is caught in the ticker's `try/catch`,
  logged under `err`, counted `failed`, loop continues. DB write errors are systemic and rethrown.
  Per results: the calendar refresh (`refreshTradingCalendar` / `ensureTradingCalendar`) now reports
  `{ status: 'no_market_data' }` on auth failure, because on a fresh install it is the first
  market-data call.
- **Why:** With no credentials every ticker fails identically; 25 WARNs would report a configuration
  state as a broken run. Everything else follows the batch failure-isolation rule.
- **Alternatives considered:** consult `settings.getCredentialStatus()` first; treat auth like any
  per-ticker failure.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`, `plans/us-121/refactor-phase-results.md` (Layer 7)

### ADR: The calendar store's window widens to cover the rank window

- **Decision:** `READ_LOOKBACK_DAYS` 45 → 400, `READ_LOOKAHEAD_DAYS` 2 → **70** (plan said 50;
  raised in refactor because monthly candidates reach +70 days), `REFRESH_LOOKBACK_DAYS` 120 → 420.
  `needsRefresh` also returns true when stored `first_day` is later than `now − READ_LOOKBACK_DAYS`
  (upgraded installs refetch once). Refresh interval (7 days) and lookahead (400) unchanged.
- **Why:** 252 sessions ≈ 365 calendar days + up to ten stale sessions + the expiration horizon.
  The "read never fetches" rule is untouched.
- **Alternatives considered:** a second, wider read function; computing the window from
  `iv30_reading` + `iv30_gap` alone.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`, `plans/us-121/refactor-phase-results.md` (Layer 1)

### ADR: Recompute is driven by `engine_version`; the run recomputes stale-version rows before it fetches

- **Decision:** `IV30_ENGINE_VERSION` lives in `core/iv30.ts` and is stamped on every row.
  `recomputeIvHistory` re-runs `iv30FromInputs` over rows behind the current version (or all when
  forced), rewriting `iv30` and version, with no provider request; unrecomputable rows are counted
  and left with a WARN. `collectIvHistory` calls it for the ticker before computing missing sessions.
  Selection defects need a refetch; the no-refetch guarantee covers what stored inputs reproduce.
- **Why:** Metrics are derived on read, so correcting `iv30` corrects everything; version stamping
  makes the upgrade automatic and idempotent.
- **Alternatives considered:** a Settings button; recompute on app start.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`

### ADR: The e2e seam is a fake provider that synthesises bars from a programmed IV series

- **Decision:** `FakeMarketDataProvider.getOptionDailyBars` prices every requested symbol for every
  fixture session with the real `core/black-scholes.ts` pricer at that session's target IV (flat
  surface), so the real engine inverts back to exactly the programmed number. Fixture from
  `WHEELBASE_FAKE_IV_SERIES` at boot, replaceable via `_test:iv-series-set`; every bar request is
  recorded. `fake-ivr.ts`, `WHEELBASE_FAKE_IVR`, `_test:ivr-set-outcomes`, `_test:ivr-fetch-log` are
  removed; the fake clock moves to `integrations/fake-clock.ts`.
- **Why:** The story names the seam ("fake option and underlying bars fed to the real engine");
  ~6 KB per ticker fixture vs ~80 KB literal bars; no pricer copy in `e2e/`.
- **Alternatives considered:** literal bars from `e2e/`; seeding `iv30_reading` directly (skips the
  parts the story wants exercised).
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`, `plans/us-121/contracts/test-iv-history.md`

### ADR: Barchart is retired from code and schema, not just from the read path

- **Decision:** Delete `barchart-ivr-scraper.ts`, `fake-ivr.ts` (+ tests), the `_test:ivr-*` outcome
  channels and preload entries; drop `ivr_snapshot` in migration 016. Keep the `ivr-collect` job
  name, `ivr:collect-now`, `ivr:snapshot-updated`, the `IvrOnDemand` port and Settings "Refresh IVR
  now". Supersedes `barchart-as-canonical-ivr-source`.
- **Why:** Dead code our change created; Barchart's rank is a different quantity, never comparable,
  stale within two weeks. Planning review (2026-09-20) chose to drop the table; the Linear scenario
  was reworded to "Barchart readings are removed on upgrade".
- **Alternatives considered:** keep scraper as fallback; keep `ivr_snapshot` for provenance; rename
  `ivr:*` channels.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`

### ADR: The pricer uses `Number`; `Decimal` is applied at the storage boundary only

- **Decision:** `core/black-scholes.ts` computes in doubles (Abramowitz–Stegun normal CDF,
  bisection on `[0.001, 10]` for 100 iterations). Results become 4-dp TEXT via `Decimal`
  `ROUND_HALF_UP`; rank/percentile rounding uses `Decimal`.
- **Why:** An implied volatility is a model output, not money; `decimal.js` has no worthwhile
  normal CDF.
- **Alternatives considered:** `Decimal` throughout; a Black–Scholes dependency.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`

### ADR: An absent rank carries a display-only reason; process state lives in memory, coverage is derived

- **Decision:** Reasons `pending`, `no_market_data`, `failed` are process state in a
  `createIvRunState()` closure (`services/iv-run-state.ts`), one instance created in `index.ts` and
  shared by the batch, on-demand path and both read paths. Callers own transitions: `markPending`
  before each ticker's turn (on-demand does it synchronously before its first `await`), `settle`
  after; `collected`/`up_to_date` clear the entry; the batch marks remaining targets
  `no_market_data`. `insufficient_history` (carrying `coverage`, `window`, `required`) is derived on
  read; `not_collected` otherwise. Precedence: `pending` > `no_market_data` > `failed` >
  `insufficient_history` > `not_collected`; a published reading always wins. Display-only.
  **Per results (authoritative):** the reading/absence pair is carried as a union (`IvRankPair`)
  through main, preload and renderer, so both-null is unrepresentable; `IvrCell` / `ReadingNote`
  take one `ivr` pair prop. (research.md's "sibling field rather than discriminated union" was the
  planned shape, superseded in the Gate D fix cycle.)
- **Why:** `null` hid five situations; two are actionable. In-memory state is honest by
  construction on relaunch.
- **Alternatives considered:** persisting run state; deriving `failed` from reading age; letting the
  reason affect the verdict.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/research.md`, `plans/us-121/data-model.md` §3b,
  `plans/us-121/refactor-phase-results.md` (Verification loop — Gate D)

### ADR: Today counts as complete only 45 minutes after its close (`BAR_SETTLE_MINUTES`)

- **Decision:** A session is "required" only once its bars have settled: close + 45 min. The
  scheduled `ivr-collect` run fires at close + 60.
- **Why:** Code review B2 — a run minutes after 16:00 pulled still-forming daily bars, and a stored
  reading is never re-probed, freezing a partial VWAP. SPY-style options trade until 16:15 and free
  SIP bars lag.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/refactor-phase-results.md` (Code review 1), `docs/us-121-implementation.md`

### ADR: On-demand pushes per ticker on every settle; the batch pushes once per run

- **Decision:** `IvrOnDemand` takes `onSettled` (fires after every settle, any outcome) and pushes
  `ivr:snapshot-updated` for that ticker. `collectIvHistoryBatch` takes `onCompleted`, fired exactly
  once per run from a `finally` (normal end, abort, no-credentials, rethrown DB error) with payload
  `{ ticker: null }`.
- **Why:** Layer 6 bug — only `collected` pushed, so a card stuck on "Computing IV history". Code
  review B1 — a per-ticker batch push re-ran the screener (a chain pull per ticker) N times.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/refactor-phase-results.md` (Layer 6; Code review 1)

## Schema Changes

### `iv30_reading`

- **Change:** new table
- **Columns / fields:** `underlying`, `session` (ET day), `method` (default `'daily_vwap'`),
  `engine_version` INTEGER, `observed_at` (session close), `iv30`, `underlying_vwap`,
  `expiration_tier` CHECK IN (`weekly`,`monthly`), `near_expiration`, `near_strike`,
  `near_call_vwap`, `near_call_trades`, `near_put_vwap`, `near_put_trades`, six nullable `far_*`
  columns (NULL together), `rate`, `dividend_yield`. PK `(underlying, session, method)`; index
  `idx_iv30_reading_underlying_session_desc (underlying, session DESC)`. Rows never deleted on aging out.
- **Source:** `plans/us-121/data-model.md` §1

## Acceptance Criteria

Verbatim scenario names (Linear OPT-27; one `it()` each in `e2e/iv-history.spec.ts`):

- IV rank is computed from the app's own IV history
- IV percentile is computed alongside IV rank
- The IV range behind the rank is reported with it
- A reading outside the window's range is clamped
- A flat window withholds rank but not percentile
- A corrected engine recomputes every derived metric from stored inputs
- Reading IV metrics makes no market-data request
- A reading is withheld while the window is too sparse to trust
- A young history is withheld the same way
- Adding a ticker does not wait on its backfill
- One ticker's backfill failure leaves the others intact
- Missed sessions are caught up by the next daily run
- An untraded strike is skipped for its neighbour
- Thin weeklies fall back to the monthly expirations
- A day with no tradeable ATM pair is left as a gap
- Today's reading is computed the same way as the history
- Bar requests never name the current calendar day as their end
- Today's reading is available the same evening
- Barchart readings are removed on upgrade
- No market-data credentials leaves IV rank unavailable, not broken
- The computed rank drives the screener floor — 29 is excluded / 30 is included / n/a is treated as it is today when IVR is unavailable
- Expirations too near expiry are excluded from IV30 — 3 DTE excluded / 6 DTE excluded / 7 DTE used / 9 DTE used / 14 DTE used

## Decisions & Tradeoffs

- `ivHistoryDeps(...)` extraction rejected: the two callers' lifecycles around the shared input
  differ (on-demand marks pending synchronously before its calendar await; batch rethrows
  `SqliteError` and marks remaining targets on `no_market_data`). (refactor-phase-results Layer 5)
- Read path split into `iv-history-read.ts` so "reading makes no market-data request" holds by
  construction. (Layer 3/4)
- `inputsOf` returns `null` for a half-written far set → counted `unrecomputable` on purpose.
- `collectIvHistory` returns `failed` (WARN `iv_history_calendar_unavailable`) when the calendar
  cannot place `now` — not in the plan.
- `insufficient` coverage counts window sessions only (60 stored rows incl. anchor → coverage 59).
- Gap-only ticker reads `insufficient` with coverage 0, not `not_collected` (code review B3).
- A gap row is replaced only through `persistIvHistory`; `collectIvHistory` never re-probes a
  gapped session.
- `fetchPages<Page>` extracted in `alpaca-market-data.ts` (third pagination copy); `followPages`
  option preserves the chain's single-page rule.
- `SessionProbePlan` carries `underlying` and `strikes`; strike walk returns the inverted IV point
  (no second inversion, no cast).
- Range/percentile line appears once at the end of `ivrTooltipCopy`'s body (plan's extra tooltip
  span dropped to avoid duplication).
- `setIvRankFloor`, `goToBench`, `seriesEnding` shared from e2e helpers.
- e2e "after close" clock is close + 60 min ET (was pinned to 21:00Z, which breaks under EST).
- `e2e/ivr-collector.spec.ts` "Re-running within the same session overwrites the existing row"
  retitled — rerun is now `up_to_date` and leaves the row unchanged.
- `cheerio` in `package.json` is unused under `src/` — noted, not removed.
- Out of scope: reviving the scraper; a paid vendor; minute/hour-bar methods; a rate series or
  dividend model; IV − HV, IV change, term structure; screening/alerting on percentile/range;
  intraday IV; backfilling beyond the rank window; charted IV history; bench card layout rework;
  renaming `ivr:*` channels or the `ivr-collect` job.
- Tidy-up pass (post-review), from `refactor-phase-results.md`:
  - Leftover "IVR snapshot" naming renamed: `collectIVRSnapshots` / `CollectIVRSnapshots*` →
    `collectIvHistoryBatch` / `CollectIvHistoryBatch*`; `ivr-snapshots.ts` → `iv-rank-lookup.ts`;

  - `Clock` lives in `src/main/dates.ts` (used by `fake-clock.ts`, `ivr-collector.ts`,

  - The lookup is now `IvRankPair` (`ivRank` / `ivRankAbsence`); `IvRankLookup` and `toIvRankPair`
