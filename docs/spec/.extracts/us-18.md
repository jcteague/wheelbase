# Extract: us-18

## Summary

"Replace the ten-column positions table with one card grid. Each wheel renders as a card with
its live price, option mark, unrealized P&L, one leg block, ledger footer, and an action strip
that mirrors the position's open management-queue rows; a PMCC renders as a minimal card in the
same shell until US-108 replaces it. Done means the 22 named e2e cases pass, every pre-existing
list spec stays green with only one header assertion rewritten, and the table, its cells and
its CSS are gone." Linear story: OPT-30 / US-18 (Linear is the authority; the archive
`docs/epics/04-stories/US-18-position-cards.md` is its reconciled source). Mockup:
`mockups/epic-09-pmcc-dashboard.mdx` (`WheelCard`, `PositionsGrid`, `CardShell`, `ActionStrip`,
`LegTag`, `FootCell`, `PriceBlock`, `QUEUE_ACTION`); `mockups/us-18-position-cards.mdx` is
history only. Blocks US-108 (OPT-16). Why: "US-108 needs a PMCC card and rejects a mixed
table-plus-card dashboard, so the wheel had to become a card first. A card also answers the
trader's real question ('which position needs me today?') faster than reading across a row: the
action strip puts the queue's verdict on the position itself." The only main-process change is
an additive `ruleCode` on the `alerts:list` queue item; **no migration**. As shipped, all 22
e2e cases (S1–S21, S13a/b) pass in `e2e/position-cards.spec.ts`. (Source: `plans/us-18/plan.md`
Summary; `plans/us-18/research.md`; `docs/us-18-implementation.md`.)

## Architecture Decisions

### ADR: The wheel strip mirrors the management queue, not re-evaluated predicates

- **Decision:** "`rankWheelActions(rows, legContext)` in `src/renderer/src/lib/card-actions.ts`
  maps a position's open `ManagementQueueItem`s (by `ruleCode`) to `{ glyph, statusWord, tone,
  rank, summary }`, drops `PROFIT_TARGET` and any code without a lane entry, and sorts by rank.
  The summary is the queue's text verbatim." As shipped, a pure `actionsByPosition(rows,
  ctxById)` in the same file groups the queue by position (each position's own quote price and
  strike feed the tone), so `PositionsListPage` stays wiring-only. "Dismissed rows are absent
  from the queue, so they are absent from the strip."
- **Why:** "the mockup assigns exactly this to US-18 ('its strip text is the queue summary word
  for word'); dismissals are honoured for free; no second copy of the thresholds. US-108's PMCC
  strip is a live derivation by its own story's choice; both share `ActionStrip` and the lane
  numbers."
- **Alternatives considered:** "evaluating `evaluatePosition` in the renderer from quotes
  (duplicate inputs, ignores dismissals); parsing summaries for the rule (fragile)."
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/research.md` § Architecture Decisions; `plans/us-18/plan.md` Area 6
  Refactor; `docs/us-18-implementation.md` § 2

### ADR: `ruleCode` joins `ManagementQueueItem`

- **Decision:** "`listManagementQueue` selects `a.rule_code AS rule_code`; `mapQueueRow` sets
  `ruleCode`; `ManagementQueueItem.ruleCode: RuleCode`." Preload already aliases the schema type
  (US-106), so no preload edit.
- **Why:** "the only honest way to know which rule a row is; additive and already stored." "The
  card maps a row to its lane by rule, never by parsing the summary." "The change is identical to
  the one on the unmerged `wb-107` branch, so the two merge cleanly."
- **Alternatives considered:** "a separate `alerts:list-by-position` channel (a second read path
  for the same rows)."
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/research.md` U1 + ADR; `plans/us-18/data-model.md` § 1;
  `docs/us-18-implementation.md` § 1

### ADR: Wheel lanes are a registry keyed by `RuleCode`

- **Decision:** As shipped, "`WHEEL_LANES` is a registry keyed by `RuleCode`"
  (`Readonly<Partial<Record<RuleCode, WheelLane>>>`), each entry `{ glyph, statusWord, tone:
  (ctx) => CardTone, rank: (tone) => number }`. Lane numbers are exported constants
  `LANE_RED_ASSIGNMENT = 1`, `LANE_GOLD_ASSIGNMENT = 2`, `LANE_EXPIRING = 4`,
  `LANE_MANAGEMENT_WINDOW = 5`; "Lanes 0 and 3 are reserved for US-108's ✕ and ◆".
  `rankWheelActions` sorts stably by lane.
- **Why:** Registry pattern, no `if/return` chain; data-model § Type Design: "the lane registry is
  `Partial<Record<RuleCode, Lane>>`, so a typo in a lane key is a compile error." Named lane
  constants "so US-108 imports them for ✕ 0 / ◆ 3."
- **Alternatives considered:** plan.md Area 2 and data-model § 2 specified "an ordered array …
  like the alert `RULES`"; superseded by the keyed record (a lane cannot name an unknown rule or
  appear twice).
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/plan.md` Area 2; `plans/us-18/data-model.md` § 2, § Type Design;
  `docs/us-18-implementation.md` § 2

### ADR: STRIKE_PROXIMITY tone comes from the live quote vs the put strike

- **Decision:** "tone is red when the live quote price is strictly below the put strike
  (`Decimal(price).lt(strike)`), gold otherwise or when the quote is missing." Red → lane 1,
  gold → lane 2.
- **Why:** "The row carries no 'in the money' flag." "The engine's summary already says `— now in
  the money` for the same condition (`priceDirection` → `'below'`), so the two agree."
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/research.md` U3

### ADR: One `CardShell` and `ActionStrip` for both strategies

- **Decision:** "primitives live in `src/renderer/src/components/position-card/` (`CardShell`,
  `CardHeader`, `PriceBlock`, `ActionStrip`, `LegBlock`, `LegTag`, `CardFooterCell`), typed on
  plain props (`tone: 'red' | 'gold'`, `accent: WheelPhase | 'PMCC'`), with no knowledge of queue
  rows or list items." As shipped, the set also includes `CardFooter` and shared class strings in
  `styles.ts`, and `CardAccent = StrategyPhase<'WHEEL'> | 'PMCC'`.
- **Why:** "US-108 reuses them unchanged for the PMCC card; the mockup draws both cards from the
  same pieces."
- **Alternatives considered:** "a single `PositionCard` with branches (the wheel card would carry
  PMCC concerns)." Also rejected at refactor: "no `PositionCardBase` abstraction."
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/research.md` ADR; `plans/us-18/plan.md` Areas 3 and 5;
  `docs/us-18-implementation.md` § 3

### ADR: `WheelCardDisplay` is a discriminated union of card states

- **Decision:** As shipped, `deriveWheelCardDisplay(item, snapshot, quote, { closed,
  profitTargetDefault })` in `src/renderer/src/lib/wheel-card-display.ts` returns "a
  discriminated union of three states": `closed | shares | option`. `WheelCard.tsx` "only
  composes; every figure comes from the display."
- **Why:** "A closed card cannot carry a TARGET badge or a live P&L, and a shares card cannot
  carry an urgent DTE: those combinations do not type-check (`expectTypeOf(...).not.toExtend`
  guards them)."
- **Alternatives considered:** data-model § 3 sketched a single interface with independent fields
  (`pnl | null`, `pnlSubline`, `targetReached`, `pnlPercent`, `maxProfit`, `targetPercent`,
  `dteUrgent`, `priceSubline`, `mark`) — superseded.
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/data-model.md` § 3; `docs/us-18-implementation.md` § 4

### ADR: The PMCC row becomes a minimal card, replaced by US-108

- **Decision:** "`PmccCard` (minimal) renders in the same shell: teal accent, PMCC badge, state
  label, price block, one `LegBlock` per open leg with a `—` mark, `—` P&L with `no valuation
  yet`, basis and premium footer. No strip, no TARGET, no flag." Next-decision DTE =
  `short?.dte ?? long.dte` ("same rule as `position-order.ts`"). As shipped, the state label is
  `LEAPS + short call open`, or `No short open` between short calls.
- **Why:** "mixed table-plus-card is rejected by US-108; the grid must have one presentation the
  day it ships. Everything shown is what `PmccPositionRow` shows today."
- **Alternatives considered:** "keeping `PmccPositionRow` inside a one-row table (two
  presentations)."
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/research.md` ADR; `plans/us-18/data-model.md` § 4;
  `docs/us-18-implementation.md` § 5

### ADR: Delete the table cells rather than wrap them

- **Decision:** "`PositionRow`, `PmccPositionRow`, `OptMidCell`, `UnrealizedPnlCell`,
  `positionRowStyles.ts`, their tests, `TABLE_COLUMNS` and the `.wb-position-row*` CSS are
  removed." `PriceCell` was also deleted (its only consumers were the two rows). "`lib/option-display.ts`
  helpers stay."
- **Why:** "the cells are `<td>`s and cannot live in a card; keeping them dead contradicts the
  orphan rule."
- **Alternatives considered:** "keeping them as an alternate view (rejected by the reconciled
  story)."
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/research.md` ADR; `plans/us-18/tasks.md` Sequencing note;
  `docs/us-18-implementation.md` § Removed

### ADR: The page subscribes to the queue a second time (TanStack dedupe)

- **Decision:** "`useManagementQueue` is already called by `ManagementQueue`; TanStack Query
  dedupes a second subscriber on the same key, so `PositionsListPage` calls it too and groups
  items by `positionId`. Only wheel items receive rows; the minimal PMCC card has no strip."
  Grid props carry `actionsByPosition: ReadonlyMap<string, CardAction[]>`.
- **Why:** "Grouping is done once at the page; cards never see the queue."
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/research.md` U2; `plans/us-18/data-model.md` § Type Design

### Card-action types and functions (renderer)

- **Type:** other (pure renderer lib)
- **Shape:**

  ```typescript
  // src/renderer/src/lib/card-actions.ts
  export type CardTone = 'red' | 'gold'
  export interface CardAction {
    glyph: '▲' | '◷' // US-108 adds '✕' | '◆' via the same shape
    statusWord: string // 'STRIKE PROXIMITY' | 'CALL BREACHED' | 'EXPIRING' | 'MANAGEMENT WINDOW'
    tone: CardTone
    rank: number
    summary: string // the queue row's text verbatim
  }
  export type QueueRowForCard = Pick<ManagementQueueItem, 'ruleCode' | 'summary'>
  export type QueueRowForPosition = Pick<ManagementQueueItem, 'positionId'> & QueueRowForCard
  export interface LaneContext { price: string | null; strike: string | null }
  export const WHEEL_LANES: Readonly<Partial<Record<RuleCode, WheelLane>>>
  export const LANE_RED_ASSIGNMENT = 1, LANE_GOLD_ASSIGNMENT = 2, LANE_EXPIRING = 4, LANE_MANAGEMENT_WINDOW = 5
  export function rankWheelActions(rows: QueueRowForCard[], ctx: LaneContext): CardAction[]
  export function formatActionLine(action: CardAction): string // `${glyph} ${statusWord} · ${summary}`
  export function actionsByPosition(rows: QueueRowForPosition[], ctxById: ReadonlyMap<string, LaneContext>): ReadonlyMap<string, CardAction[]>
  ```

  | `ruleCode`            | glyph | statusWord          | tone                 | rank  |
  | --------------------- | ----- | ------------------- | -------------------- | ----- |
  | `COVERED_CALL_BREACH` | ▲     | `CALL BREACHED`     | red                  | 1     |
  | `STRIKE_PROXIMITY`    | ▲     | `STRIKE PROXIMITY`  | red if ITM else gold | 1 / 2 |
  | `EXPIRATION_IMMINENT` | ◷     | `EXPIRING`          | gold                 | 4     |
  | `MANAGEMENT_WINDOW`   | ◷     | `MANAGEMENT WINDOW` | gold                 | 5     |

  "Rows with any other code (`PROFIT_TARGET`, `EARNINGS_PROXIMITY`, `PMCC_*`) are dropped."
- **Source:** `plans/us-18/data-model.md` § 2, § Type Design; `docs/us-18-implementation.md` § 2

## Decisions & Tradeoffs

- **`no bid` kept on the card (U4).** "The story's AC omit it, but US-33's `no bid` indicator is
  asserted by `e2e/option-pnl.spec.ts` AC-8 and still describes real data. Resolution: the leg
  mark keeps the `no bid` subtext via `hasNoBid`; the ⚠ is suppressed in that case as today." As
  shipped: "`no bid` wins over the wide-spread warning: a zero bid always reads as wide."
  (`plans/us-18/research.md`; `src/renderer/src/lib/wheel-card-display.ts`)
- **Closed cards keep the short phase badge (U5).** "`position-management.spec.ts` expects `CSP ✓`
  in the closed card, so the card keeps `PhaseBadge variant="short"`." Closed cards show `—` for
  price, mid, DTE and P&L; "snapshots and quotes are not requested for closed items."
  (`plans/us-18/research.md`)
- **Closed leg text.** As shipped, "wheel complete" only for `WHEEL_COMPLETE`, else "position
  closed" ("a CSP bought back closed the position early"). Closed cards stay clickable.
  (`docs/us-18-implementation.md` § 4)
- **Row-wide ≤ 7 DTE highlight dropped (U7).** "The mockup highlights only the DTE and shows the
  flag; the card edge is reserved for action tones. Resolution: follow the mockup; drop the row
  CSS." (`plans/us-18/research.md`)
- **Page test mocks the cards (U6).** Mock `WheelCard` and `PmccCard` "with the same
  `mock-quote-{T}` / `mock-snapshot-{T}` stubs, plus a `mock-actions-{T}` stub so the
  queue-to-card grouping is asserted at the page level." (`plans/us-18/research.md`)
