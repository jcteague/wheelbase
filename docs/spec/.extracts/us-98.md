# Extract: us-98

## Summary

US-98 ages an IV-rank reading against **completed New York trading sessions** and
**known intervening earnings**, so a stale reading can no longer pass as current. A
shared verdict (`fresh` / `aging` / `stale` / `predates_earnings`, or nothing at all)
is computed once at the service boundary and consumed by both the screener's ranked
table and — once US-96 lands — the watchlist Signal. Unusable readings stay _visible_
but are excluded from every decision: they cannot satisfy an IV gate, and the
screener's `iv_rank_floor` is never applied to them, so a stale value can neither
promote nor exclude a candidate.

**Implementation status at extraction: partial.** `plans/us-98/tasks.md` shows Layers
1–3 Red/Green complete (trading calendar, freshness engine, collector guard, earnings
split, assessed read service, screener contract, shared IVR cell, test clock). Still
open: every `/refactor` task, Layer 4 (watchlist Signal — blocked on US-96, which is
not in this checkout), Layer 5 (the 13-case E2E acceptance suite), and Layer 6
(verification + QA). Two prerequisite gates are also unticked: trader validation of
the tier boundaries, and landing US-96.

## Architecture Decisions

### ADR: Freshness is measured in completed exchange sessions, not calendar days

- **Decision:** A reading's age is the number of session closes strictly after the
  close of the session the reading was observed in, up to and including `now`. A
  Friday-close reading is age 0 all Monday morning; a holiday does not age it; an
  early close (13:00 ET) is a full session.
- **Why:** Elapsed calendar days and UTC-date arithmetic both mis-age readings across
  weekends, holidays and DST. Black Friday's 14:00 ET already belongs to Friday's
  session; ignoring early closes is simply incorrect.
- **Alternatives considered:** Weekend-only heuristics (fails AC3); derived holiday
  formulas (still need exception and early-close data).
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/research.md`, `plans/us-98/data-model.md`

### ADR (emerged in implementation): The exchange calendar is fetched and cached, not checked in

- **Decision:** The plan called for an immutable checked-in 2025–2028 NYSE table
  (`core/trading-calendar-data.ts`). The implementation instead fetches the venue's own
  calendar through `BrokerProvider.getMarketCalendar` and caches it in a new
  `trading_session` table. `core/trading-calendar.ts` stays pure and reasons over a
  `TradingCalendar` value the caller supplies.
- **Why:** Per the module comment, a hand-maintained holiday table expires silently;
  observed-holiday conventions have exceptions and unscheduled closures happen, so
  sessions are treated as facts to cache rather than rules to derive. It also keeps
  `src/main/core` free of I/O and of a data file that must be renewed annually.
- **Alternatives considered:** The plan's offline table (rejected in implementation);
  a broker-only live lookup with no cache (rejected — reads must never hang on the
  broker, and a brokerless watchlist trader must still get answers).
- **Source:** `migrations/015_create_trading_session.sql`,
  `src/main/services/trading-calendar-store.ts` (implementation; supersedes the offline
  decision in `plans/us-98/research.md`)

### ADR: Unknown coverage is a distinct answer from "closed"

- **Decision:** `SessionLookup` is a three-way union — `open` / `closed` /
  `unavailable`. Coverage is derived from the rows actually stored
  (`MIN(date)..MAX(date)`), and closures are persisted explicitly as a NULL `close_at`
  so a day nobody fetched reads as _unknown_ rather than silently as a closure. Outside
  coverage, IVR assessment degrades to "no usable reading"; the collector logs and
  continues best-effort.
- **Why:** Never certify freshness from a guessed calendar. An exhausted calendar
  silently disables every freshness judgement, so the store warns 30 days before
  coverage runs out.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/research.md`, `plans/us-98/data-model.md`,
  `migrations/015_create_trading_session.sql`

### ADR: Earnings invalidation outranks the age tiers

- **Decision:** A known last print strictly after the reading's session day and at or
  before today (ET) marks the reading `predates_earnings` and unusable — however new it
  is. This override is evaluated _before_ time expiry, so an earnings-invalid reading
  older than ten sessions keeps its explanatory state rather than collapsing to
  "no reading".
- **Why:** The story states earnings override the tier table. A print resets the IV
  regime, so a pre-print reading describes a world that no longer exists.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/research.md`, `plans/us-98/data-model.md`

### ADR: Date-only earnings cannot order a same-session-day print

- **Decision:** The invalidation predicate is _strictly later day_. A print dated the
  same day as the reading's session does not invalidate it, and does not automatically
  become invalidating tomorrow. The UI never displays a "current through earnings"
  affirmation.
- **Why:** Finnhub's calendar is date-only; BMO/AMC certainty is out of scope. Absence
  of an override is not evidence of earnings safety.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/research.md` ("Limitation"), `plans/us-98/data-model.md`

### ADR: Only usable readings enter the engine; assessed readings are overlaid afterwards

- **Decision:** `services/screener.ts` builds the engine's `ivRanks` map from _usable_
  readings only, then overlays the full assessed reading onto each ranked row after
  `rankCandidates`. `FILTERS`, the yield/score formula, rank ordering and exclusion
  reasons are untouched.
- **Why:** A stale value must stay visible while being absent from both positive gates
  and negative filters — it must neither satisfy `iv_rank_floor` nor trigger it.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/research.md`, `plans/us-98/contracts/screener-results.md`

### ADR (emerged in implementation): `usable` is derived at both ends, not carried on the wire

- **Decision:** The contract specified a `usable: boolean` field on `IpcIvRank`. The
  implementation drops it: `AssessedIvRank` carries `{ value, observedAt,
ageTradingDays, state }`, and usability is derived from `state` by `isUsableState()`
  in main and by the tone rule in `IvrCell`.
- **Why:** One rule rather than two that can drift apart; a fixture cannot express an
  inconsistent `state`/`usable` pair.
- **Source:** `src/main/core/ivr-freshness.ts`, `src/preload/index.d.ts`
  (implementation; amends `plans/us-98/contracts/screener-results.md`)

### ADR (emerged in implementation): Assessment returns a three-state result, not `null`

- **Decision:** `assessIvRank` returns `{ status: 'assessed' | 'expired' | 'unreadable' }`
  rather than the planned `AssessedIvRank | null`. Both non-assessed states render as
  "no IV rank", but only `unreadable` is logged as degradation at the service boundary.
- **Why:** Ordinary ageing-out and a corrupt row / unreachable calendar look identical
  on screen; collapsing them to `null` let a corrupt row degrade the screen in silence.
- **Source:** `src/main/core/ivr-freshness.ts`, `src/main/services/ivr-snapshots.ts`
  (implementation; amends `plans/us-98/data-model.md`)

### ADR: The collector's non-trading-day guard moves from the broker clock to the calendar

- **Decision:** `collectIVRSnapshots` no longer reads `getMarketStatus` and no longer
  applies a UTC weekend heuristic. It refreshes the cached calendar (when a broker is
  configured), then asks `getTradingSession` for today's ET date: `closed` skips with
  the existing `market_closed` summary; `unavailable` logs and continues best-effort;
  `open` collects. `fetchMarketStatusOrNull` and `isTradingDay` are deleted.
- **Why:** The old heuristic treated a missing or failing broker as "assume trading
  day", so a holiday collection still ran and overwrote the previous good reading. The
  broker argument is now optional and used only to _refresh_ the calendar.
- **Supersedes:** the `ivr-non-trading-day-guard-in-collector` ADR.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/plan.md` (Area 3), `src/main/services/ivr-collector.ts`

### ADR: The daily collector run is what keeps the calendar cache ahead of today

- **Decision:** The IVR collector refreshes the calendar before reading it, at most
  once every 7 days, fetching 120 days back through 400 days ahead. A broker outage
  leaves the previous rows untouched and reports `failed` — the batch it gates still
  runs.
- **Why:** No separate calendar-sync service or cadence change. Boundary I/O degrades
  rather than rejects (CLAUDE.md batch-job rule).
- **Source:** `src/main/services/trading-calendar-store.ts`

### ADR: Earnings history is split from the next print

- **Decision:** The Finnhub fetch is widened to 30 days back and returns next and last
  _independently_ (`{ status: 'read', next, last }` or `{ status: 'unavailable' }`).
  A nullable `last_earnings` column is added to the existing `earnings_date` row. A
  shared read-through `getEarningsCalendar` returns both; `getEarnings` becomes a
  next-only projection of it, so existing alert/US-70 callers are unchanged and no
  ticker is requested twice.
- **Why:** The next date cannot serve as history for a ticker added after its print.
  A successfully fetched last date must survive a failed cache write, which a separate
  DB-only reader could not deliver.
- **Alternatives considered:** A new endpoint; rolling next into last; a historical
  event table (all unneeded or miss the post-print-addition case).
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/research.md`, `plans/us-98/contracts/earnings-calendar-read.md`

### ADR (emerged in implementation): A passed `next_earnings` is recovered as last-print knowledge

- **Decision:** `storedLastPrint` treats a cached `next_earnings` that is now in the
  past as evidence of a print that happened, taking the later of it and
  `last_earnings`.
- **Why:** Returning only the stored `last` dropped that knowledge, so the freshness
  engine judged a pre-print reading on age alone and a stale high IVR read as usable
  the morning after a print. Recovering it costs no fetch.
- **Source:** `src/main/services/earnings-dates.ts`

### ADR: `null` and `undefined` last-earnings mean different things, and neither is a safety claim

- **Decision:** `null` = a successful feed read established an empty lookback;
  `undefined` = missing or unavailable knowledge. Both fall back to the time tiers
  alone. Migrated NULL rows are indistinguishable from checked-empty ones from this
  column, so they are treated as "no known print" and never as proof of a 30-day check.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/research.md`, `plans/us-98/data-model.md`

### ADR: All date comparisons are on the Eastern calendar day

- **Decision:** Feed request bounds, next/last selection, cache-freshness comparisons
  and the invalidation predicate all compare ET days via `etDateOf` /`etDayPlus`.
  Conversion uses native `Intl.DateTimeFormat` with an explicit `America/New_York`
  zone — no added timezone dependency, no string slicing, no locale reparsing. IVR
  snapshot persistence keeps its existing UTC-day deduplication.
- **Why:** Every stored date came out of the feed bucketed by Eastern day. A host-local
  midnight is a different day for part of every evening, which on a UTC host rejected
  each print-day read as already-past and discarded the last-print knowledge with it.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/research.md`, `src/main/services/earnings-dates.ts`

### ADR: Unusable readings are muted data quality, not validation errors

- **Decision:** A shared `IvrCell` renders every IVR reading. Fresh is a bare value
  with no age or date qualifier; Aging and Stale append `· Nd`; `predates_earnings`
  adds a "predates earnings" caption; absent renders `n/a`. Unusable states are muted
  (`text-wb-text-muted`), overriding any richer per-surface value tone. The observation
  date lives in the tooltip only, formatted in ET.
- **Why:** Muted is neutral data quality; red would read as a validation error. The
  permanent `44 (Aug 7)` date suffix is retired — the age qualifier carries the signal.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/research.md`, `plans/us-98/data-model.md`,
  `src/renderer/src/components/IvrCell.tsx`

### ADR: One shared test clock, injected through composition

- **Decision:** `createFakeIvrCollaborators()` is resolved once in `index.ts` _before_
  screener IPC registration, and its clock is shared by the collector and the screener
  (and, once US-96 lands, the watchlist). A test-only clock setter sits behind the
  existing `NODE_ENV=test` `test-ivr` seam. Production never accepts a clock through
  public IPC; the optional clock is a composition dependency, never renderer input.
- **Why:** A holiday test must collect on the preceding open day, advance the clock,
  then view — seeding by collecting _on_ the holiday must no longer work.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/plan.md` (Area 8), `plans/us-98/contracts/screener-results.md`,
  `src/main/index.ts`

### ADR: The watchlist reuses US-96's snapshot and Signal rather than adding an endpoint

- **Decision (not yet implemented):** The watchlist snapshot's IVR field becomes the
  same assessed reading, computed with `getAssessedIvrByUnderlying` at the same request
  clock Signal uses. `deriveSignal` treats an unusable or absent reading as _unknown_,
  never as a failed numeric comparison, and any required unknown gate prevents "Entry
  ready". Labels: stale → "IV too old to judge", `predates_earnings` → "IV predates
  earnings", missing/expired → "IV unavailable". A ticker with no IV condition gains no
  new gate.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/contracts/watchlist-ivr.md`

## Schema Changes

### `trading_session` (new table)

- **Change:** new table
- **Columns / fields:** `date TEXT PRIMARY KEY` (Eastern calendar day, `YYYY-MM-DD`),
  `close_at TEXT` (ISO instant of the close; **NULL means the exchange was closed**),
  `source TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'alpaca'`.
- **Notes:** One row per calendar day in the fetched range, _not_ one per trading day —
  storing closures explicitly is what lets coverage be derived as
  `MIN(date)..MAX(date)`, so an unfetched day reads as unknown rather than as a
  closure. `close_at` already reflects early closes, so there is no separate half-day
  concept.
- **Source:** implementation — not in `plans/us-98/data-model.md`
- **Migration file:** `migrations/015_create_trading_session.sql`

### `earnings_date.last_earnings` (new column)

- **Change:** new nullable column on the existing one-row-per-ticker table
- **Columns / fields:** `last_earnings TEXT` — the latest print strictly before today
  that the feed reported. Legacy NULLs mean "no known print", never proof of a checked
  lookback. No backfill and no cadence change.
- **Source:** `plans/us-98/data-model.md`
- **Migration file:** `migrations/014_add_last_earnings.sql`

## Acceptance Criteria

Every Gherkin scenario is an AC; each is to become one verbatim-named E2E test in
`e2e/ivr-staleness.spec.ts`.

- **AC1** — A reading from the last close shows without an age qualifier
- **AC2** — Friday's close is still fresh on Monday morning
- **AC3** — An exchange holiday does not age a reading
- **AC4** — An aging reading shows its age but stays usable
- **AC5** — A stale reading is muted and cannot satisfy an IV condition
- **AC6** — An expired reading is indistinguishable from no reading
- **AC7** — An earnings print invalidates a reading regardless of age
- **AC8** — A print before the observation does not invalidate the reading
- **AC9** — Missing earnings knowledge falls back to the time tiers alone
- **AC10** — A stale IV rank never blocks a candidate from ranking
- **AC11** — The IV-rank floor is not applied to a stale reading
- **AC12** — The IV-rank floor is not applied to an expired reading
- **AC13** — Signal refuses to claim entry readiness on an unusable reading

Coverage status: **all 13 are verified** by `e2e/ivr-staleness.spec.ts`, one
verbatim-named test each, all passing. AC5 and AC13 were added once US-96 landed and gave
them a surface to assert on — the "Signal" and "Entry ready" they were written against
shipped as the per-gate verdict and the "Meets criteria" section.

## Decisions & Tradeoffs

- **Tier boundaries** are Fresh 0–1, Aging 2–3, Stale 4–10, Expired over 10 completed
  sessions (`FRESH_MAX_AGE` / `AGING_MAX_AGE` / `STALE_MAX_AGE` in
  `core/ivr-freshness.ts`). The plan flags these as _proposed_: trader validation is a
  prerequisite gate that is still unticked.
- **Zero is a value.** An IVR of 0 is a real reading, distinct from no reading.
- **Nothing is persisted.** Assessment is recomputed per request; no assessed state is
  stored, and there is no new polling or collection triggered by rendering.
- **No renderer date arithmetic.** The cell receives a verdict; it never computes age.
- **Read never fetches.** `readTradingCalendar` is pure DB — a screen must not hang on
  the broker. Only the daily collector refreshes.
- **Read window is bounded** to 45 days back / 2 ahead, comfortably past
  `STALE_MAX_AGE`, so an ancient snapshot cannot make the read walk months of rows.
- **Coverage warning at 30 days** — an exhausted calendar disables every freshness
  judgement silently, so the store warns while there is still time to act.
- **`Intl.DateTimeFormat` is constructed once per module**, not per row: the cell
  renders on every row of every screen.
- **The plan's `core/trading-calendar-data.ts` was never created** — the fetched-and-
  cached calendar replaced it.
- **US-96 is not deferred.** Both watchlist ACs remain mandatory; the plan explicitly
  rejects skipping or `todo`-ing them.
