# Extract: us-64

## Summary

US-64 adds a backend batch job that, for every watchlist ticker, pulls the **put** side
of the option chain within a DTE window through the `MarketDataProvider` adapter and
returns per-strike `bid / ask / mark / delta / openInterest / volume` plus the quote
timestamp. It extends the US-39 adapter so chain results carry per-strike identity and
real OI/volume, adds pure DTE/filter helpers, and orchestrates a failure-isolated
fan-out that distinguishes `provider_unavailable`, `no_options_listed`, and per-ticker
`data_unavailable` from a genuine zero-candidate result. Backend-only: no IPC handler
and no renderer surface (US-65 consumes the service; US-66 displays it). Nothing is
persisted — market data is transient.

## Architecture Decisions

### ADR: Extend the shared adapter to carry per-strike identity + OI/volume on chain results

- **Decision:** Add `OptionChainQuote = OptionSnapshot & { contractId, strike,
  expiration, contractType }` and change `getOptionChainSnapshot(filter)` to return
  `Promise<OptionChainQuote[]>`. In the Massive provider, extend the internal chain
  result type with `details` (`strike_price`, `expiration_date`, `contract_type`,
  `ticker`), `open_interest`, and `day.volume`, and add `mapChainResult` that reuses
  `mapSnapResult`'s money logic while populating identity and real OI/volume. The
  single-contract `getOptionSnapshot` and its `OptionSnapshot` return are unchanged.
- **Why:** The ACs cannot be satisfied without per-strike identity and real OI/volume,
  and the story mandates sourcing chains *through the adapter* (no direct Massive calls
  in the screener). A dedicated `OptionChainQuote` keeps identity fields **required**
  (type-safe for screening) rather than optional-everywhere.
- **Alternatives considered:** (a) Add optional `strike?/expiration?/contractId?` to
  `OptionSnapshot` — rejected: makes every screening consumer null-check fields that are
  always present for chains. (b) Build a screener-local type and re-parse OCC symbols in
  the service — rejected: duplicates parsing, bypasses the adapter seam, and still can't
  recover OI/volume the provider dropped.
- **Source:** `plans/us-64/research.md`

### ADR: Screener chain-pull is a pure-core + service split; no new IPC in US-64

- **Decision:** Pure helpers (`dteWindowToExpirationRange`, `toCandidateStrikes`,
  `isTradeableStrike`, `classifyChainFailure`) live in `src/main/core/candidate-chain.ts`
  (no I/O, no logging). Orchestration (`pullWatchlistChains`) lives in
  `src/main/services/candidate-chains.ts` and does the watchlist read, per-ticker
  provider fan-out, and degrade. **No IPC handler or renderer surface is added.**
- **Why:** US-64 produces raw chain data consumed in-process by the US-65 scorer; the
  trader-facing refresh/display is US-66. Adding IPC now would be speculative surface
  (Simplicity-First). The split mirrors `costbasis.ts`/`lifecycle.ts` (pure) vs
  `watchlist.ts`/`market-data.ts` (I/O) and keeps DTE math + filtering unit-testable
  without a provider.
- **Alternatives considered:** (a) Add a `screener:pull-chains` IPC now — rejected as
  speculative. (b) Put everything in one service function — rejected: the DTE-range and
  filter logic are pure and deserve isolated tests.
- **Source:** `plans/us-64/research.md`

### ADR: Three no-result states classified from raw chain length + error code

- **Decision:** Per ticker, in its own `try/catch`: a non-empty array → `status: 'ok'`
  with filtered `strikes` (may be empty after dropping untradeable strikes — still
  `ok`); an empty array → `status: 'no_options_listed'`; a throw →
  `status: 'data_unavailable'`, logged at **debug** for `not_found` and **warn** for
  provider-level codes (**error** for a non-`MarketDataError` throw). Overall status is
  `provider_unavailable` **iff** no ticker answered (no `ok` and no
  `no_options_listed`) **and** at least one failure was provider-level.
- **Why:** Directly satisfies the failure/empty ACs and the "outage ≠ zero results"
  invariant. `MarketDataError.code` is the only reliable classification signal.
  Distinguishing raw-empty (no listed options) from filtered-empty (options exist but
  all untradeable) matches the "no options listed" vs normal-screen semantics.
- **Alternatives considered:** Mirroring `fetchOptionSnapshots`'s single `unavailable`
  boolean — rejected: it conflates outage with legitimately-empty and can't express
  `no_options_listed`.
- **Source:** `plans/us-64/research.md`, refined by the pre-merge code review (see
  *Decisions & Tradeoffs*).

### ADR: DTE window is a parameter with a 30–45 default; not coupled to US-67 settings

- **Decision:** `pullWatchlistChains` accepts an optional `window: { min, max }`
  defaulting to `DEFAULT_DTE_WINDOW = { min: 30, max: 45 }`, and an optional
  `currentDate` (default `new Date()` at the service boundary; injected explicitly in
  tests and by callers). `dteWindowToExpirationRange` uses `date-fns` `addDays` +
  `format('yyyy-MM-dd')` to derive `expirationFrom`/`expirationTo`.
- **Why:** US-67 (persisted screening defaults) is not a dependency of US-64.
  Parameterizing lets US-65/US-67 pass persisted criteria later without US-64 reaching
  into the settings store now. Injecting `currentDate` keeps the DTE math deterministic
  and testable.
- **Alternatives considered:** Read the US-67 settings store directly — rejected: US-67
  may not be implemented, and it couples this story to a non-dependency.
- **Source:** `plans/us-64/research.md`

### ADR: DTE arithmetic and formatting share a single local-timezone basis

- **Decision:** `dteWindowToExpirationRange` does both the day arithmetic and the
  `yyyy-MM-dd` formatting in the local timezone (`date-fns` `addDays` + `format`), the
  same basis as the shared helpers in `src/main/dates.ts`.
- **Why:** DTE is counted from the trader's own calendar day. Mixing bases — local
  `addDays` with UTC formatting — shifts the entire window by a day whenever the run
  happens outside UTC's calendar day (e.g. any evening in a negative-offset zone),
  silently excluding the expiration the trader expects at the near edge.
- **Source:** pre-merge code review (superseded the plan's UTC-formatting note).

### ADR: Per-ticker chain fan-out is concurrency-bounded

- **Decision:** `pullWatchlistChains` fans out through a bounded worker pool
  (`CHAIN_FETCH_CONCURRENCY = 4`) rather than an unbounded `Promise.all`.
- **Why:** Each ticker's chain is a fully-paginated walk, so an unbounded fan-out over a
  large watchlist bursts hundreds of requests and earns a 429 from a healthy provider.
  `rate_limited` classifies as provider-level, so the burst would surface to the trader
  as a fake outage and reproduce on every retry.
- **Source:** pre-merge code review.

## Contracts

### `MarketDataProvider.getOptionChainSnapshot` (extended)

- **Type:** provider adapter method (not IPC)
- **Shape:**

```typescript
// request — unchanged
type OptionChainFilter = {
  underlying: string
  expirationFrom?: string // "YYYY-MM-DD"
  expirationTo?: string // "YYYY-MM-DD"
  type?: 'put' | 'call'
  strikeFrom?: string
  strikeTo?: string
  limit?: number
  cursor?: string
}

// response — CHANGED: OptionSnapshot[] -> OptionChainQuote[]
type OptionChainQuote = OptionSnapshot & {
  contractId: string // "AAPL260918P00190000" (O: prefix stripped)
  strike: string // 4dp decimal string
  expiration: string // "YYYY-MM-DD"
  contractType: 'put' | 'call'
}
```

  US-64 calls it with `{ underlying, expirationFrom, expirationTo, type: 'put' }`. An
  empty array is a **normal** response (no listed options in-window), not an error.
- **Error codes:** the existing fixed `MarketDataError` set — `auth_failed` (401/403),
  `not_found` (404), `rate_limited` (429 after `MAX_RETRIES`), `network_error`,
  `unknown`. No story-specific codes. Consumed in-process and classified rather than
  converted to the IPC envelope.
- **Source:** `plans/us-64/contracts/option-chain-snapshot.md`

  `src/main/integrations/massive-market-data.ts`,
  `src/main/integrations/fake-market-data.ts`

## Schema Changes

None recorded. US-64 persists nothing — market data is transient, and no migration was
added. The integration test seeds the existing `watchlist` table (migration
`012_create_watchlist.sql`) in an in-memory DB.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Pull put chains for each watchlist ticker: each AAPL/MSFT put strike within 30–45 DTE
  carries bid, ask, mark, delta, open interest, and volume; `mark = (bid + ask) / 2`
  HALF_UP 2 dp; each strike carries the quote timestamp from Massive.
- A single ticker failing does not suppress the others: with a 404 for XYZ, AAPL and
  MSFT candidates are still produced, XYZ is marked "data unavailable", and the failure
  is logged at debug level.
- Whole-provider outage is distinguished from zero results: the screener reports
  "market data unavailable" and does **not** report "no candidates found".
- A ticker with no listed options is skipped, not failed: XYZ is marked "no options
  listed" and remains on the watchlist.
- Zero-bid and one-sided strikes are dropped, because no reliable mark can be computed.

## Decisions & Tradeoffs

- **`mark` is never recomputed.** The candidate row copies the adapter's `mid`
  (`computeMid` = `(bid + ask) / 2` HALF_UP 2 dp) rather than recomputing from a float,
  so money rounding lives in exactly one place.
- **Strike strings are 4 dp**, matching the codebase-wide TEXT money convention
  (`legs.strike`, `watchlist.own_below_price`), so chain strikes compare directly
  against persisted ones. (The plan originally specified 2 dp; corrected pre-merge.)
- **Optional provider payload blocks are tolerated.** Chain entries for never-traded or
  thinly-quoted strikes omit `last_trade`, `last_quote`, and `greeks` entirely; they map
  to a zeroed quote (which the tradeability filter then drops) instead of throwing and
  discarding the whole underlying's chain. A response with no `results` array at all is
  an empty chain, not a provider error.
- **Fake-provider fixtures are keyed by OCC symbol**, and per-strike identity is derived
  from that key rather than assumed present on the value — every e2e spec predating the
  chain endpoint seeds bare `OptionSnapshot` values there.
- **Batch completion is logged at INFO** (status, ticker count, ok count, unavailable
  count), consistent with `ivr-collector` and `evaluate-alerts`; per-ticker detail stays
  at debug/warn/error.
- **Failure isolation** follows the alert-evaluation-failure-isolation ADR: each ticker
  is fetched in its own `try/catch`; the batch never rejects.
