## Gaps between today and the story

1. No combined page; no verdict anywhere; no per-entry quote/IVR/earnings read for the watchlist.
2. `expired` collapses to `null` before the renderer, so `exp` cannot render and cannot be told from `n/a`.
3. `IvrCell` has no ring or tooltip; no Tooltip primitive exists.
4. `registerWatchlistIpc` has no provider or clock; `index.ts` creates the fake-IVR clock **after** registering watchlist IPC.
5. `useAddToWatchlist` invalidates only the watchlist key, so a new stock would not be screened until a manual refresh.
6. Five e2e specs and their helpers target `#/screener`.

## Architecture Decisions

### ADR: One combined page at `/watchlist`; the `/screener` route is retired

- **Decision:** `WatchlistPage` becomes the combined bench per mockup B. Remove the `/screener` route, `ScreenerPage` (+ test), the Screener nav item, `SCREENER_PAGE_TITLE`, and `PAGE_TITLES['/screener']`. `ScreenerResultsTable` and `ScreenerExcludedSection` (+ tests) are deleted; `ScreenerCriteriaStrip`, `ScreenerStateCard`, `ScreeningCriteriaSheet`, `EarningsBadge`, `PromotedFormChrome` flow are reused unchanged.
- **Why:** The user chose concept B and chose to retire rather than redirect. Reading the bench today requires joining two pages by eye; the join is the feature. A redirect would keep dead surface alive for one stale hash.
- **Alternatives considered:** Keep both pages (rejected: two sources of truth for "what should I trade"); redirect `/screener` → `/watchlist` (rejected by the user; an unmatched hash already renders an empty `Switch` today, so this is consistent with existing behaviour).

### ADR: A separate read-only `watchlist:snapshot` IPC, joined to `screener:results` in the renderer

- **Decision:** Add `watchlist:snapshot` (no payload) → `services/watchlist-snapshot.ts::buildWatchlistSnapshot(getProvider, db, { currentDate })` returning one row per entry with `quote | null`, `ivRank: AssessedIvRank | null`, `earnings`, and the per-gate `verdict`. It does **not** pull chains. The page runs two TanStack queries (`watchlistQueryKeys.snapshot`, `screenerQueryKeys.results`) and a pure renderer helper `lib/bench.ts::buildBench(snapshot, results)` produces `{ meets, waiting }`.
- **Why:** The screener is a heavy chain pull with its own provider-outage state; the snapshot is a light read that must stay useful during a chain outage (AC "Market data unavailable degrades verdicts, not rows" shows IVR rings from the local store). Keeping them separate preserves `screener:results`'s contract and its e2e coverage. The join is display grouping, and the `verdict-pure-compute` ADR already places pure routing logic in `src/renderer/src/lib/`.
- **Alternatives considered:** One fat IPC that runs the screener and the snapshot together (rejected: couples the light read to the heavy one, and the outage state would blank the whole page); compute the join in the main process (rejected: it would need chain results inside the snapshot service, same coupling).

### ADR: Gate verdicts are a pure main-process engine, `src/main/core/watchlist-signal.ts`

- **Decision:** `evaluateEntry(entry, snapshot, now)` returns `{ price, iv, earnings }` gates, each `{ verdict: 'met' | 'unmet' | 'unknown' | 'none', label: string | null }`. Rules: **price** — no `ownBelowPrice` → none; quote missing → unknown "Price unavailable"; `price ≤ target` → met; else unmet `Price $X above $Y target`. **IV** — no `ivrTrigger` → none; reading null or `expired` → unknown "IV unavailable"; `stale` → unknown "IV too old to judge"; `predates_earnings` → unknown "IV predates earnings"; usable and `value ≥ trigger` → met; else unmet "IV low". **Earnings** — `postEarningsOnly` false → none; next print within 7 calendar days (ET) → unmet `Earnings in N days`; `unavailable`/`none`/`unknown` → unknown "Earnings date unknown"; else met. `decimal.js` for money comparison; `date-fns` for the day count. No I/O imports.
- **Why:** The story and the US-98 `plans/us-98/contracts/watchlist-ivr.md` contract both call for a pure derivation callable by future alerting; the IV-gate table there is adopted verbatim. Computing at the snapshot's request clock guarantees the IVR age the trader sees and the age the verdict used are the same.
- **Alternatives considered:** Derive verdicts in the renderer from `useStockQuotes` + snapshot (rejected: two clocks, and price would come from a different transport than the verdict); reuse the story's original Signal chip labels ("4.9% away") (rejected by the user in favour of B's reason text).

### ADR: `expired` becomes a fifth `IvRankState` carried on the assessed reading

- **Decision:** `IvRankState` gains `'expired'`; `assessIvRank` returns `{ status: 'assessed', reading: { …, state: 'expired' } }` instead of `{ status: 'expired' }`; `IvRankAssessment` loses the `expired` variant. `isUsableState` is unchanged, so `services/screener.ts::usableIvRanks` still excludes it and the engine never scores on it. `IpcIvRank.state`, `ScreenerIvRank.state`, and `IvrCell` widen to match. `getAssessedIvrByUnderlying` no longer maps expired to `null`.
- **Why:** The US-98 story specified `exp` with a quarter ring as a collection-health hint distinguishable from `n/a`; the implementation collapsed it. The user asked for the ring treatment, which needs the distinction. `unreadable` stays `null` — a corrupt row is still "no reading".
- **Alternatives considered:** Keep `null` and render `exp` from a side channel (rejected: two shapes for one fact); add an `expired: boolean` beside `state` (rejected: US-98's "one rule, not two that can drift").

### ADR: `IvrCell` gains a freshness ring and a Radix tooltip

- **Decision:** `IvrCell` renders `<span data-testid="ivr-cell" data-ivr-state=…>` containing the numeral (`exp` when expired; `formatIvrValue` otherwise) and `<FreshnessRing state>` (inline SVG, fill fraction `{fresh:1, aging:.75, stale:.5, expired:.25, predates_earnings:0}`, stroke colours via `wb` tokens as SVG attributes, gold track + centre dot for predates_earnings). The whole cell is a `TooltipTrigger`; `TooltipContent` (new `src/renderer/src/components/ui/tooltip.tsx` from `pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add tooltip`, which installs `@radix-ui/react-tooltip`) shows the tier title, and body copy from a pure `ivrTooltipCopy(reading)` helper in `lib/ivr-tooltip.ts`. The `· Nd` suffix stays for aging/stale so the existing US-98 assertions hold; `title` is dropped in favour of the tooltip, `aria-label` keeps the age + ET observation date. `n/a` keeps `data-ivr-state="empty"` and no ring.
- **Why:** Radix portals the tooltip, so `SectionCard`'s `overflow:hidden` cannot clip it (the mockup had to fake this with `position: fixed`). The ring is a step function of tier so equal treatment looks equal (US-98 story).
- **Alternatives considered:** CSS `title` only (rejected: no tier title, no styling); the mockup's hand-rolled fixed tooltip (rejected: inline positional style, no focus management); Popover primitive (rejected: click-to-open, wrong affordance).

### ADR: The bench is a snapshot, not a stream

- **Decision:** Prices on the bench come from the snapshot IPC (REST `getStockQuotes`, per-ticker isolated), refetched on Refresh, criteria save, add/remove, and window focus. The page does not subscribe to `market-data:set-stock-quote-tickers`. Day change = `(price − prevClose) / prevClose`, one decimal, signed; `prevClose` null → no change line.
- **Why:** The verdict must be computed on the same price the trader sees; streaming ticks would make price and verdict drift between refreshes. The header `MarketStatusPill` already conveys freshness (project rule: no per-row timing indicators).
- **Alternatives considered:** `useStockQuotes` streaming for price + snapshot for the rest (rejected: two transports, verdict drift).

### ADR: Quote fetch isolation is shared, not duplicated

- **Decision:** Extract `services/screener.ts::readUnderlyingPrices`'s per-ticker isolated fetch into `services/underlying-quotes.ts::fetchIsolatedStockQuotes(provider, tickers): Promise<Map<string, IpcStockQuote>>` (concurrency 4, one `try/catch` per ticker, warn-log on failure) and use it from both the screener (price-ceiling path, mapping to `price`) and the snapshot service.
- **Why:** Same hazard (429s, one bad ticker), same behaviour; a second copy is guaranteed drift. Screener behaviour is unchanged because the criteria-gated early return stays in the screener.
- **Alternatives considered:** Call `fetchStockQuotes` once for all tickers (rejected: a single failing symbol would drop every price).

### ADR: Refresh and mutations invalidate both queries

- **Decision:** Header "Refresh" calls `refetch()` on both the snapshot and screener queries. `useAddToWatchlist` and `useRemoveFromWatchlist` additionally invalidate `watchlistQueryKeys.snapshot` and `screenerQueryKeys.results`. The criteria sheet's `onSaved` already refreshes the screener; the page also refetches the snapshot.
- **Why:** AC "Add stock … NVDA appears under Stocks of interest after the re-screen" and AC "Refresh re-screens the bench and prices update". One button, one mental model.
- **Alternatives considered:** Leave new stocks "Not screened yet" until manual refresh (the mockup's prototype copy; rejected as a needless extra click).

### ADR: Preserve US-63 seams; rank number and earnings badge move onto the meets card

- **Decision:** Cards keep `data-testid="watchlist-row-{ticker}"` (plus `data-bench-section="meets|waiting"`), the ticker span keeps `watchlist-ticker`, the ✕ keeps `watchlist-remove-{ticker}` on every card, and condition tags keep `watchlist-tag` (rendered in the detail panel as `GateBadge`s + static tags). A meets card shows its rank (`#n`, or `—` when the candidate's earnings verdict demoted it — `RankCell` logic from `ScreenerResultsTable`) and the existing `EarningsBadge` when `candidate.earnings.status !== 'clear'`. The Review trade button is `data-testid="bench-review-{ticker}"`.
- **Why:** US-63's e2e stays green without edits; US-66's rank-order and US-70's demotion ACs keep an assertable surface after the ranked table goes.
- **Alternatives considered:** Remove only from the detail panel (rejected: US-63 remove e2e would need a select step); drop rank numerals (rejected: loses US-70's "carry a rank number" signal).

### ADR: E2E re-pointing strategy

- **Decision:** `e2e/screener-helpers.ts`: `goToScreener` → `goToBench` (`#/watchlist`, wait for `h1:has-text("Watchlist")`); `launchScreener`/`relaunchScreener`/`reloadScreener` keep their names and options. New page queries: `meetsTickers(page)` (cards under `[data-bench-section="meets"]` in DOM order), `cardReason(page, t)` (`[data-testid="watchlist-reason"]`), `selectCard(page, t)`, `detailPutMetrics(page)` (`dl` under `bench-detail-put`), `ivrCell(page, t)` (moved from `ivr-staleness.spec.ts`, reads `data-ivr-state` and the numeral). `rankedTickers` → `meetsTickers`; `rowCells`/`rowRank` → `cardRank`; `excludedReason` → `cardReason`; `promoteRow` → `selectCard` + click `bench-review-{t}`. Specs `screener-results`, `screener-earnings`, `screening-criteria`, `promote-to-trade`, `ivr-staleness` are edited in place; their `describe` titles keep the story ids.
- **Why:** Those specs encode still-valid ACs of US-66/67/68/70/98 on a page that has moved; re-pointing keeps the coverage and makes regressions in the move visible.
- **Alternatives considered:** Delete and rewrite from scratch (rejected: loses the AC mapping comments and fixtures).
