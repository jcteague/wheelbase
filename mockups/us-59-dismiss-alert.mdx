# US-59: Dismiss Alert

_As a wheel trader triaging my queue, I want to dismiss an alert I have reviewed so the queue stays focused while preserving an audit trail._

## Acceptance Criteria States

- **open**: active queue item with a dismiss affordance beside the quick action
- **confirm**: explicit confirmation state so the trader understands the alert will be hidden until the condition clears
- **dismissed**: open queue is cleared and the dismissal record remains visible in history
