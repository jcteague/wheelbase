# US-67 (revised): Screening Criteria Sheet

_As a wheel trader, I want to edit the screener criteria from the Screener itself — in a sheet, like every other form — so I can tune filters against the results I am looking at instead of navigating to Settings._

## What changed from the original US-67

The original story said "extend the existing settings surface — do not create a standalone page", and the first mockup (`us-67-screening-defaults.mdx`) put the criteria form on the Settings page. This revision moves the same criteria into a right-hand **sheet opened from the Screener**, matching `AssignmentSheet`, `RollCspSheet`, `CallAwaySheet`, and `CloseCcEarlySheet`.

Three consequences worth deciding on before build:

1. **Two entry points, one form.** A `⚙ Criteria` button in the Screener page header, plus a clickable criteria-summary strip above the results. The empty state's "Loosen your delta band or DTE window in Screener settings" copy becomes an in-place **Adjust criteria** button — no navigation away.
2. **Save re-screens.** The primary action is `Save & re-screen`, not `Save screening criteria`. Co-locating the form with the results is the whole point of the move, so the results behind the sheet must refresh on save. This is a real behavioral change from the Settings version, where saving only persisted.
3. **Persistence is unchanged.** The criteria still live in the same settings store the alert thresholds use (US-57), still validated by the same Zod schema on both sides of IPC. Only the surface moves. Settings keeps alert defaults and credentials; it no longer owns screening criteria.

## Acceptance Criteria States

- **entry** — sheet closed. Shows both entry points: the `⚙ Criteria` header button and the criteria-summary strip (`Δ 0.20–0.30 · DTE 30–45 · OI ≥ 500 · Spread ≤ 10% · Earnings Exclude`) sitting above the ranked results.
- **default** — sheet open over the results at every shipped default. The scrim starts to the right of the sidebar (`SheetOverlay` is `left-[200px]`), so nav stays reachable. Save enabled.
- **invalid** — delta band `0.30 → 0.20` and DTE window `45 → 30` both inverted, each with an inline error, and the footer **Save disabled** — the cross-field min-less-than-max validation.
- **optional** — earnings handling switched to `Flag only`, with the IV-rank floor (30) and price ceiling ($75) both toggled `On`, showing the enabled look of the two optional inputs.
- **saved** — sheet dismissed, a `Screening criteria saved` confirmation on the page, the summary strip updated to `Δ 0.15–0.20 · DTE 40–45`, and the table **re-screened** against the new band (KO 0.18 / MSFT 0.16 / AAPL 0.19, all 44 DTE; excluded count rises 4 → 7).

Colors, fonts, and chrome come from `src/renderer/src/index.css` (`--wb-*`). Sheet anatomy mirrors `src/renderer/src/components/ui/Sheet.tsx` — `SheetOverlay` / `SheetPanel` / `SheetHeader` / `SheetBody` / `SheetFooter`. Panel is **460px** rather than the 400px default: the paired min/max inputs need the extra width. Yield math is internally consistent: `period = mark / strike`, `annualized = period × 365 / DTE`, `score = annualized ÷ delta`.
